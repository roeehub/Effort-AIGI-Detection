## Executive summary

The contract's rank-1/rank-2 ordering between P8A_REFERENCE_STEP5000 and
SLOT_A_ANCHOR_AWARE_STEP3500 on `lockbox_real_fpr` is a one-video tiebreak
(25 vs 26 false positives out of 1361 real lockbox videos) at the contract-frozen
τ. The paired bootstrap shows the delta is statistically indistinguishable from
zero: the 95% CI on delta is [−0.0081, +0.0096], centered at +0.0006, and
P(delta > 0) = 0.519. On any independent draw of the lockbox cohort the rank
ordering on this metric is roughly a coin flip.

Given that the +30 percentage-point lockbox_fake_recall gap (0.387 → 0.688) is
real (253 fakes is a large enough cohort to be confident in the direction), and
that all other contract gates (dev primary FPR, dev stress FPR, dev fake macro
recall floor) are MET by Slot A v2, the contract's lex-first ordering on a
sampling-noise tiebreak is, in my view, an artifact of the rank rule rather
than substantive evidence to keep P8A as the deployment ckpt.

## The mechanism I propose

The contract's `_promotion_summary_sort_key` (in
`arena/score_teams_promotion_contract.py`) ranks ckpts first by `lockbox_real_fpr`
and only then by `lockbox_fake_recall`. This is a sensible default when ranks are
robust — a 5pp lockbox_real_fpr gap is rarely sampling noise. But on cohorts where
both ckpts are near-zero on the rank-1 axis (here both ≈ 1.8–1.9%), the rank
becomes hostage to a single-video swing, and the +30pp lockbox_fake_recall lift
gets blocked by a 1-video tiebreak.

**What this opinion explains**: why the contract picks P8A despite Slot A v2's
massive fake-recall lift; why the gap looks tight on the scorecard.

**What this opinion does NOT explain**:
- Whether Slot A v2 has chronic-FP regressions on other axes the lockbox metric
  doesn't capture (e.g., Roy_D cluster regression noted in
  `project_band_shortcut_ood_hypothesis_2026-05-16.md`: dev FPR 29→81%; that is
  not visible in `teams_real_all_lockbox` but is in `teams_real_dor_dev` and
  related readouts). The Bar-1-MET finding is conditional on the contract's
  primary lockbox-real cohort; it does NOT prove Slot A v2 is generally
  invariant.
- Whether the manual canary (memory entry
  `project_overnight_3_packets_2026_05_20.md`) showing Slot 1 (a different but
  related run from the same packet sequence) regresses through training is also
  true for Slot A v2 step3500 specifically. Slot A v2 ≠ Slot 1 — this needs
  per-ckpt manual canary readout, not extrapolation.

**What I am unsure about**: the lockbox_fake cohort is 253 videos. The +30pp lift
on lockbox_fake_recall (0.387 → 0.688) corresponds to 98 → 174 fakes caught — a
delta of 76 videos. That is comfortably above noise (a similar bootstrap there
would likely show CI not covering 0), but I did not compute it in this job. If
the reviewer wants a symmetric statistical test, that bootstrap is the obvious
complement to the present one.

## Self-correction log

### Retraction 1 — initial misreading of the "union of 3 lockbox suites" scope

I started by interpreting the task's "union of contract lockbox-real suites:
teams_real_all_lockbox, teams_real_lighting_extreme_lockbox,
teams_real_poor_quality_lockbox" as three distinct cohorts whose video sets
needed to be unioned. Counting rows in each per-video report and checking
video_id set membership showed:

- lighting_extreme (207 vids) ⊂ teams_real_all_lockbox (1361 vids)
- poor_quality (22 vids) ⊂ teams_real_all_lockbox (1361 vids)
- set-union of the three = 1361 vids (identical to teams_real_all_lockbox)

The stress suites are strict subsets of the parent suite. The contract's
`lockbox_real_fpr` metric itself is computed on `teams_real_all_lockbox` alone
(verified in `promotion_winner.json` → `contract.lockbox_real_suite`). The
literal task-scope and the contract-scope coincide. I documented this in FACTS
§Inputs and §Method §Scope-of-bootstrap rather than re-running an "Analysis B"
that would have been identical to Analysis A. Lesson: when a task says "union",
verify cardinality before assuming distinct cohorts.

## Counter-experiments that would falsify this opinion

1. Bootstrap on `lockbox_fake_recall` returns a CI that also covers 0. If true,
   the +30pp gap is also noise and the case for shipping Slot A v2 collapses.
2. Slot A v2 step3500 shows lockbox_real_fpr > 0.05 on any chronic-identity
   readout suite (Roy_D, dor_shkedi, PC_Generator, bla_bla_chow). If true, the
   "Bar-1-MET in aggregate" finding is masking a per-identity regression and the
   rank-1 ordering may be substantively right for the wrong reason.
3. A second independent contract scorecard run (different RNG, different score-
   path stochasticity) yields ranks 0.0184 / 0.0191 reproduced to within ±0.001
   AND P8A wins again — strengthens the per-video stability of the metric but
   does NOT falsify the noise claim; the bootstrap is over the cohort, not the
   model.
4. Repeating the lockbox eval on a fresh draw of the lockbox cohort (if any
   exists from a held-out shard) shows Slot A v2 rank-1 on `lockbox_real_fpr`.
   Strong supporting evidence; absence would not falsify.
