# Skill: Research Understanding

Goal:
Extract actionable ML insights from whitepapers.

Steps:
1. Identify:
   - model architecture
   - loss functions
   - training strategy
   - datasets
2. Convert into:
   - implementation blocks
   - training hypotheses
3. Highlight:
   - assumptions
   - limitations
   - missing details

Output:
- structured summary
- list of experiments to reproduce
