> **What was measured**: Three CLIP-frozen ViT-B/16 L11 CLS feature pools — dev-real
> (n=2000), lockbox-real (n=414), training-real (n=371) — were projected onto the
> 1-D axis defined by a re-fit of the D8 KLIEP-style logistic-regression
> discriminator on (dev-real, lockbox-real) features in L2-normalized CLIP-frozen
> space. Distribution overlap, Wasserstein-1, KS, and angle to the IQ-PC1 vector
> were computed.

## §1. Inputs & method

### 1.1 Inputs

| Resource | Path | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Dev/Lockbox CLIP-frozen L11 cache | `analysis/cpu_diagnostics_2026-05-12_d8_head_retrain_substrate_balanced/outputs/clip_frozen_l11__n4839.npz` | 4839 × 768 |
| Training-real CLIP-frozen L11 cache | `analysis/cpu_diagnostics_2026-05-12_d7_fpr_decomposition/_clip_l11_features.npz` | train_feats 371 × 768 |
| Eval split lookup (replay D8 sampling) | `analysis/lockbox_tagging/full_tags_2026-04-27.parquet` | 7,334 rows |
| IQ atlas | `analysis/iq_data_atlas_2026-05-08/outputs/per_frame.parquet` | 15,236 rows |

The D8 cache stacks features in the path order [4000 dev (2000 real + 2000 fake)] +
[839 lockbox (414 real + 425 fake)]. D10 filters by `label == real` to recover
the dev-real (n=2000) and lockbox-real (n=414) subsets, with seed=42
`np.random.default_rng` replay of the D8 stratified dev sampling.

The D7 training-real cache `_clip_l11_features.npz` carries `train_feats` (371
×768) and `train_basenames` (371). The training pool was originally drawn from
`analysis/iq_data_atlas_2026-05-08/_cache/train_teams_real_pool.parquet` (500
rows); 371 of these 500 were locally cached at D7 extraction time.

### 1.2 KLIEP discriminator (D10 re-fit)

`sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(C=1.0, max_iter=2000, solver="lbfgs",
n_jobs=1, class_weight="balanced")` on (X = L2-normalized 768-d CLIP-frozen
features, y = 1 if lockbox_real else 0). n_train = 2000 + 414 = 2414. Returns a
classifier with weight vector `w ∈ ℝ^768` and bias `b ∈ ℝ`. The KLIEP axis is
`proj(x) = w · (x / ‖x‖₂) + b`; the discriminator decision boundary is at
`proj == 0`.

The D10 fit differs from D8 §1.3 estimator C only in `max_iter=2000` (D8 used
5000); the rest matches D8: `LogisticRegression(C=1.0, class_weight="balanced",
n_jobs=1)` on L2-normalized concatenated features.

### 1.3 IQ-PC1 construction

For each of the 6 IQ axes `[lap_var, min_dim, color_a_dev, color_b_dev,
saturation_mean, luma_mean]`, fit `sklearn.LinearRegression(n_jobs=1)` of
z-scored axis values against the 768-d feature row. Normalize each coefficient
to a unit vector. Stack into a 6×768 matrix W. Take the first right-singular
vector `Vt[0]` from `numpy.linalg.svd(W, full_matrices=False)`. The IQ axes
were joined onto the 2000+414=2414 dev/lockbox real frame_paths via the atlas;
888 of 2414 (36.8%) had all 6 axes non-null. The 768-d IQ-PC1 was fit on those
888 frames, raw (un-normalized) CLIP-frozen feature inputs, matching the
`compute_iq_pc1` / `fit_axis_direction` recipe in
`analysis/cpu_diagnostics_2026-05-12_d6_empirical_orthogonality/run_d6.py:209-254`.

Angle between two unit vectors u, v: `degrees(arccos(|u·v|))`, range [0°, 90°].

### 1.4 Bootstrap

B=20 resamples of (dev_real, lockbox_real) with replacement. For each, refit
KLIEP, recompute the projection mean on each pool. Reported as `train_mean_std`
etc. across 20 bootstrap samples. Seed = 42.

### 1.5 Operational constraints

- `n_jobs=1` in every sklearn call (memory `feedback_sklearn_njobs.md`).
- CPU only; both feature caches were extracted previously (D7 and D8 paid the
  CLIP forward-pass cost).
- Total wall time of D10 main pipeline: 22.9 s.
- Reproducer script: `run_d10.py`.

## §8. Direct observations (numerical only)

1. KLIEP discriminator balanced training accuracy: 0.9909 (D8 §3.3 reported
   the same to 4 decimal places under `max_iter=5000`). The KLIEP axis is
   defined up to scale; `‖w‖₂ = 17.44`, `b = −1.231`.
2. Pool mean projections on the KLIEP axis: dev_real = −3.162, lockbox_real =
   +3.169, train_real = −2.401. The midpoint between dev_real and lockbox_real
   means is +0.004, i.e., approximately the discriminator's decision boundary
   at 0 (the intercept b shifts the boundary slightly off the midpoint of the
   class means because of `class_weight="balanced"` and feature normalization).
3. The training pool's mean (−2.401) is on the same side of the discriminator
   boundary as the dev pool's mean. The dev↔train_mean difference (+0.761) is
   12.0% of the dev↔lockbox mean difference (6.332).
4. The training pool overlap into dev_real's [p5, p95]: 0.7763 of 371 = 288
   frames. The training pool overlap into lockbox_real's [p5, p95]: 0.0997 of
   371 = 37 frames.
5. Decision-boundary split of training pool: 87.33% of train_real frames sit on
   the dev side (proj < 0); 12.67% sit on the lockbox side (proj ≥ 0).
6. Wasserstein-1 between train_real and lockbox_real projections (5.570) is
   7.25× the Wasserstein-1 between train_real and dev_real projections (0.769).
   Wasserstein-1 between dev_real and lockbox_real projections is 6.332.
7. KS statistic train_real vs dev_real = 0.1425 (p = 5.16e−06). KS statistic
   train_real vs lockbox_real = 0.8556 (p = 4.94e−148). KS statistic dev_real
   vs lockbox_real = 0.9843 (p < 3.5e−323).
8. The training-pool projection standard deviation (2.328) is 1.78× the
   dev-real std (1.310) and 1.88× the lockbox-real std (1.241).
9. Training-pool projection range [−6.007, +4.730] spans both the dev-real
   range and the lockbox-real range (lockbox_real range [−0.562, +4.898];
   dev_real range [−5.865, +1.977]).
10. Acute angle between KLIEP_w and IQ-PC1 = 89.71° (cos = 0.0051) on 888
    dev/lockbox frames with all 6 IQ axes joinable from the atlas. Individual
    axis-direction angles to KLIEP_w span [88.52°, 89.98°]. The 89.71° is
    robust to feature normalization choice: 4 combinations of {L2-norm,
    raw} for {KLIEP-fit, IQ-PC1-fit} give acute angles in [89.26°, 89.71°].
11. Bootstrap (B=20) train-pool mean: −2.404 ± 0.036 (CV = 1.49%); sign of the
    train mean is preserved across all 20 bootstrap iterations.

## §9. Caveats

1. **Training-pool reference size = 371 frames** (not the ~2000 originally
   requested in the D7 task brief). The 371 are the locally-cached subset of
   `train_teams_real_pool.parquet`'s 500 rows. Extracting more training
   features would have required GCS pulls outside D10's time budget; the
   bootstrap CV of 1.49% on the train-pool mean indicates the 371-frame
   estimate is stable for the first-moment statistic but does not bound the
   estimate of higher-moment quantities (especially p95 and max which sit in
   the lockbox-side tail).

2. **IQ-PC1 was fit on 888 of 2414 dev/lockbox real frames** (36.8% atlas
   coverage). The atlas `per_frame.parquet` contains a fixed 500-row sample of
   each `teams_real_all_dev` and `teams_real_all_lockbox` pool, so the
   coverage is sample-bounded, not metadata-bounded. The 89.71° angle is
   stable across feature-normalization choices (§6.3) but no bootstrap was run
   on the IQ-PC1 vector itself in D10.

3. **The dev/lockbox real feature ordering** in the D8 cache assumes the
   `clip_frozen_l11__n4839.npz` was generated under the seed=42 stratified
   sampling described in D8 §1.1 and `run_d8.py:99-116`. The D10 script
   re-runs that sampling at load time and asserts the resulting count
   (2000 dev_real + 414 lockbox_real) matches the cache feature count
   (4000 dev + 839 lockbox = 4839 total).

4. **The KLIEP discriminator was re-fit under `max_iter=2000`**; D8 used
   `max_iter=5000`. Both produce balanced accuracy 0.9909 on the joint
   training set, indicating convergence on both.

5. **`projection_per_frame.csv` is per-frame**: it carries pool, frame_id (the
   gcs_uri for dev/lockbox; the basename for train), and the scalar projection.
   2,785 rows total (2000 + 414 + 371).

6. **The IQ-PC1 angle reflects the linear-subspace alignment in the 768-d
   feature space**. The KLIEP_w direction can correlate with IQ axes at
   second order or via interactions that the linear angle measure does not
   capture. D7 §5 / §6 quantified the joint substrate+IQ+chronic predictive
   contribution of substrate_distance separately (substrate-block ΔR² ≤
   0.015 in the joint model for both P8A and T5C).

7. **The training pool here (`train_teams_real_pool.parquet`)** is the
   IQ-atlas training-real sample (n=500 in the parquet; 371 locally cached).
   Per `D9_FACTS_2026-05-12.md §4.1`, this pool maps to the
   `live-deepfake-methods-real-and-fake-frames-cropped-teams-v2` bucket
   (combined_paired.teams lane, primary training source for 191/195 R13
   yamls). It does not include the `proper_data` lane (HDTF + QCLIPS), the
   `external_training_reals` lane, or the prior-version `teams` (v1) bucket.

8. **Single-direction projection**: D10 reports overlap and Wasserstein on the
   1-D KLIEP axis only. The dev↔lockbox decomposition may have additional
   orthogonal directions of separation; KLIEP_w is the maximum-likelihood
   single LR direction under `class_weight="balanced"`. A multidimensional
   alignment (e.g., LDA after PCA) was not computed.

9. **No identity-blocking**: per memory `project_lockbox_identity_looseness`,
   dev and lockbox real pools share identity_keys (e.g., dor, PC_Generator,
   bla_bla_chow appear in both). The KLIEP discriminator may use identity-
   driven features in addition to capture-pipeline features; D10 does not
   decompose these.

10. **The `train_teams_real_pool.parquet` itself was sampled** from the larger
    training bucket without explicit guarantee of representativeness for
    intra-bucket diversity. D9 §4.1 reports 250 of 1,646 sample directories
    sampled at audit time (15.2%); the IQ atlas's 500-row training pool is a
    parallel sample and may have different identity / strategy mix. The D10
    n=371 is a third nested subsample (local-cache filter).

11. **`b = −1.2307`** combined with `‖w‖₂ = 17.4374` gives a probability-50%
    boundary on the L2-normalized feature space; the per-frame proj values
    are the log-odds offsets relative to that boundary in the LR sense, up to
    a scale of `‖w‖₂`. The numeric projection magnitudes are not directly
    interpretable as probabilities without applying the sigmoid; sigmoid of
    the projection gives `P(y=lockbox | x)`. The means {-3.16, +3.17} map to
    {σ(-3.16) = 0.041, σ(+3.17) = 0.960} as posterior class probabilities.

12. **The bootstrap (§7) resamples (dev, lockbox) with replacement** but does
    NOT resample the training pool. Training-pool variance under the
    discriminator axis would require either (a) bootstrapping the training
    pool with replacement against a fixed discriminator, or (b) extracting a
    larger training-pool reference set.
