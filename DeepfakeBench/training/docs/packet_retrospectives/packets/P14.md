### Headline finding: jitter alone is the load-bearing lever

The P14 bundle (`anchor_aware` + `pipeline_randomization` + `face_scale_jitter` at `scale_limit=0.25`) scored 0.116. The sister variant — same FT base, same data, anchor_aware + pipeline_randomization DISABLED, jitter at `scale_limit=0.50` (2× the bundle's strength) — scored 0.661. **The bundle was net-negative vs its single load-bearing component**, by ~5.7×. Two interpretations are consistent with the observation; both probably contribute:

1. **Jitter at 0.25 is undertrained against the face-pixel-area shortcut**; the bundle's other two interventions don't compensate, so the bundle's jitter half is doing all the lifting in a regime where it can't yet bite. Jitter at 0.50 (matching the t∈[0.7, 1.5] tightness range that flipped 53% of frames in the 04-27 audit per [`face_size_label_leak`](../threads/face_size_label_leak.md)) finally has enough range to bite.
2. **anchor_aware + pipeline_randomization on top of jitter create regularization conflict** in the FT-from-P8A regime — the model has to satisfy too many soft constraints simultaneously and ends up specializing on a narrow regime where most fake-method generalization collapses (other_fakes_tpr 0.020 in the bundle vs 0.591 in jitter-isolated).

Either way, the operational implication is the same: **future packets that stack anti-shortcut interventions must include single-lever ablations or risk shipping a bundle whose components mask each other**. New thread [`anti_shortcut_bundle_decomposition`](../threads/anti_shortcut_bundle_decomposition.md) captures the discipline.

### DATA_FIX outcome: Move-1 prediction empirically confirmed

`R13_P14_DATA_FIX.yaml` added `visomaster_teams_enhanced` (companion_domains=`["teams_v2"]`, include_statuses=`["teams_v2_companion"]`, the conjunction-source eval-substrate match) at family_weight 8.0 on top of the bundle. The hypothesis was that Layer-2 bucket closure would deliver the headline-recall lift the AUC ceiling licensed (≈24% under corrected contract policy per memory `project_p8a_frame_level_auc_2026-04-29.md`).

Instead, **`other_fakes_tpr` crashed from the bundle's already-bad 0.020 to 0.047** — i.e., 4.7% of held-out other-method fakes detected at calibrated τ. This is not an in-distribution lift trade; it is a *generalization collapse*. Explanation: at family_weight=8.0, the conjunction-source dominates the fake-side gradient, the model specializes on the Teams-recapture × enhancer fingerprint, and other fake methods become out-of-distribution.

This empirically reinforces memory `project_move1_bucket_gap_refuted.md`: Move 1 had already shown that P8A's frozen features rank-order viso fake-vs-real correctly across every bucket variant (probe AUC 0.92-0.998); the headline-metric magnitude was scorer-driven, not bucket-driven. DATA_FIX rode on the residual hypothesis that the eval-substrate match would lift the conjunction-source recall enough to compensate; it did not. **Recommendation: drop DATA_FIX from the active queue.** The `p14-data-fix-not-launched` open loop in [`viso_bucket_gap`](../threads/viso_bucket_gap.md) closes negatively (close criterion's recall floor not hit; superseded by the no-lift evidence).

### What the lever is NOT — face-size invariance refuted (2026-04-30 afternoon)

The two analyses ran the same afternoon. Both produced refutations of the morning's "Headline finding" interpretation paragraph above:

| Probe | Result | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Face-size invariance probe (180 frames × 5 tightnesses, 4 ckpts) | `mclioexb` flip rate **43.9%** vs P8A baseline 39.4%; median \|Δprob_fake\| **0.303** vs 0.128 (2.4× worse) | **Jitter@0.50 did NOT close the face-size leak**. The supposed face-size lever made the model *more* sensitive to crop tightness on production-honest frames, not less. |
| Domain-confusion linear probe (P8A vs SLOT2_GRL vs SLOT3_JITTER, 2-way fallback) | macro-OVR AUC 0.9999 / 0.9994 / 0.9969 | None of the three ckpts collapsed the [CLS] domain manifold. GRL didn't bite either. |
| Embedding triptych (3×3 t-SNE: ckpts × {label, capture_mode, face_size}) | gross arc geometry conserved across all 3 rows; size-quartile and capture-mode coloring patterns broadly similar | Both interventions left the [CLS] feature manifold's gross axes intact. |

The "Headline finding" paragraph above proposed two possible mechanisms for jitter@0.50's win: (1) face-pixel-area invariance, and (2) bundle regularization conflict. Mechanism (1) is REFUTED on direct measurement. Mechanism (2) survives — the bundle's drag on jitter alone is still the cleanest framing — but the framing of jitter@0.50 as "the right *dose* against the face-size leak" no longer holds.

Three candidate alternative mechanisms by which jitter@0.50 actually wins remain plausible: (a) decision-boundary / calibration shift without [CLS]-feature rearrangement; (b) intermediate-layer feature shift (the [CLS]-only probes miss it); (c) test-substrate mismatch (the 180 production-honest frames don't span the face-size range jitter@0.50 was trained against). All three are tracked as candidate probes in the new thread [`jitter_winner_mechanism_unknown`](../threads/jitter_winner_mechanism_unknown.md). The empirical recommendation to use `face_scale_jitter.scale_limit ≥ 0.50` over 0.25 still holds (jitter@0.50 wins value_composite by 5.7×); what changes is the *causal story* — future packets that stack jitter@0.50 with GRL or other levers should not assume "face-size invariance achieved" as a building block.

## Conclusions drawn in-session

*(Plan retro — conclusions are about the plan, not results.)*

- **The packet has two parallel variants tackling different layers**: P14_FT (architecturally identical to P13 except FT-from-P8A and post-fix interventions) and P14_DATA_FIX (the bucket-gap closure that the Slice-7 audit reframes as the cheaper-first lift). The two are not redundant — even after Layer 2 closes, the residual shortcut at Axis 3 Δ ~0.5 still triggers the deployment block on the modern_v2 FPR axis.
- **Move-1 (the linear-probe pre-spend gate) is the cheapest decisive step** before committing to P14_DATA_FIX plumbing. ~$0 / ~1-2 hours. It either confirms the bucket gap as dispositive (high lift expected) or downgrades it (P15 GRL keeps higher priority). **Move-1 has not been executed as of 2026-04-29.**
- **P14_DATA_FIX scorecard reads must use the corrected contract policy**, otherwise the bucket-fix lift will be invisible at the headline (the τ-tail-collapse regime would still report viso recall ~1%). Pass `--promotion_target_fake_recall_min 0.30` to the validation runner. The contract-policy v3 fix is in working tree, uncommitted as of 2026-04-29 — see [`contract_policy_bug`](../threads/contract_policy_bug.md).
- **The eval split needs care if P14_DATA_FIX wires the eval bucket directly into training**. `visomaster_enhanced_macro_dev` is 550 videos all from one Teams-recapture session; if it lands in training as-is, it stops being a deployment proxy. Split by identity, not by frame, before wiring. Caveat in `docs/relaunch_handoffs/TRAINING_EVAL_VISO_BUCKET_GAP_2026-04-29.md` § 5.1.
- **P15 GRL is reframed as still-useful-but-secondary post-Slice-7**. GRL on training-only pipelines does not teach the model the eval pipeline; expected gain is much higher *after* the bucket gap is closed. See `docs/relaunch_handoffs/P15_GRL_READINESS_NOTE_2026-04-29.md`.
