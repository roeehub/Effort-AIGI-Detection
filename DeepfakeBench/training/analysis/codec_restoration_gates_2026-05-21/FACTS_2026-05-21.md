## What the gates DID NOT test

- **Multi-frame training-time effect.** Each gate is a single-frame static-image test with seed-shuffled aug parameters. Training applies aug across ~110K augmented frames over 3500 steps and the encoder learns invariance, not score-matching of individual frames. The gates are necessary (validity of the mechanism on a known case) but not sufficient (training-time encoder behavior is the actual quantity of interest).
- **AUC preservation on a labeled panel.** Deferred to the in-flight wandb canary on the 3 launched packets (runs every 500 steps with built-in fake-recall and chronic-FP monitors).
- **Per-axis decomposition.** Already done in `analysis/teams_account_natural_experiment_2026-05-19/CHEAP_FOLLOWUPS_FACTS_2026-05-19.md` (G-channel scale dominant axis, sharpness 4th-strongest, saturation effectively zero). The codec aug bundles brightness/contrast/blur/JPEG — not isolated to any one of those.
- **Multi-account generalization.** Gates use only the 2026-05-19 natural-experiment pair (Roy_D / Guest). A multi-account capture sweep (recommended at §6.1 of the natural-experiment FACTS) would test how broadly the codec aug helps.
