# Thread: Gate alignment story

## The question

Across [RLP3](../packets/RLP3.md) through [RLP5](../packets/RLP5.md), the best training recipes hit a composite ceiling around 0.72–0.77 that the team initially read as a representation limit. Is the ceiling real, or are we measuring against a misaligned gate — pools in the evaluation that do not belong there, fake-side contamination driving up `worst_pool_fpr`, or a `worst_pool_fpr` definition that is itself wrong?

## Initial belief

Through [RLP5](../packets/RLP5.md) the composite ceiling was treated as largely training-bound. The E3 lift (`0.7232 → 0.7736`) was read as evidence more training-side intervention was needed. The `wma_failure_fake` pool was suspected as a `worst_pool_fpr` driver (the "fake-pool hygiene" framing). `avspeech` was in the real pool monitoring set by default. Hint contamination in `teams_ood_fake` was visible but not prioritized.

## What changed our mind

- **2026-04-17 WT-A bad-data freeze (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/WT-A_2026-04-17.md:38-52`, `docs/VISOMASTER_BAD_DATA_POLICY_PLAN_IMPACT_2026-04-17.md:36-60`).** The April-17 audit pinned the policy categories: 4,904 sample IDs ignored, 480 retained as `visomaster hints`, 202 retained as `visomaster hints (teams)`, 3 delete-only. The corrected paired-object total dropped from `15,532 → 10,388`; corrected train pairs from `13,294 → 8,932`; retained hint train pairs `571` (`396 + 175`). Direct-Teams contamination: `237/1348` `pair_complete` rows flagged as bad VisoMaster-through-Teams (`198` train), of which the policy retained `175` as `visomaster_hints_teams` (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/WT-A_policy_truth_artifact_2026-04-17.json`). This freeze is the root artifact every later gate-alignment claim resolves against. WT-A explicitly classified every checked-in Track-A/merged/teamsonly config as `old-semantics` (`WT-A_policy_truth_artifact_2026-04-17.json:33-37`) — same-day policy-corrected retrains are not honest under the tracked tree alone.
- **2026-04-17 lane-semantics redesign (`docs/POLICY_AWARE_SOURCE_REDESIGN_2026-04-17.md:79-86`).** The "frozen lane semantics" table named four lane kinds explicitly: `visomaster_hints` (weak-signal), `visomaster_hints_teams` (weak-signal), `teams` (mixed — direct Teams data after removing policy-retained weak rows), `visomaster_teams_enhanced` (mixed diagnostic — mostly `clean_fallback`, no Teams-enhanced fake emission). This is the source of the rule used every subsequent packet — hints are not method-faithful supervision; `teams_ood_fake` should be hint-excluded; per-method VisoMaster reasoning on retained rows is invalid.
- **2026-04-17 WT-F future schema (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/WT-F_2026-04-17.md:23-37`).** Six canonical exact lanes (`proper_real_{clean,teams}`, `proper_visomaster_{clean,enhanced_clean,teams,enhanced_teams}`) frozen. The *forward* deployment-target gate framework is laid out before any proper-data is loaded — the `proper_real_teams_*` / `proper_fake_teams_*` suite slots inside the WT-E manifest pre-name the slots that will later become the deployment gate (`WT-F_2026-04-17.md:39-56`).
- **2026-04-21 first-evidence of validation-pool composition risk (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET2_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-21.md:31-42` and the RLP3 follow-up read at `R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-21.md:24-29`).** RLP2 ran enhanced-vs-unenhanced proper-data arms (`RLP2_03` adds `proper_visomaster_enhanced_teams`; `RLP2_04` adds `proper_visomaster_enhanced_clean`) but `val_holdout` for `RLP2_02` had **zero enhanced-proper content** (the unenhanced-only val pool measured against runs trained with enhanced data). The RLP3 plan codifies this as the structural reason the in-session "enhanced hurts" call had to be downgraded to muddled: *"`val_holdout` for `RLP2_02` contains zero enhanced-proper content, so we are measuring against an unenhanced-only validation slice. We cannot separate 'enhanced training hurts the enhanced slice we don't measure' from 'enhanced training hurts the unenhanced slice we do measure.'"* The pattern — *the gate composition decides what conclusion is even readable* — is the same gate-alignment mechanic later corrected at scale in [RLP6](../packets/RLP6.md). RLP2 is the first place it bit. The RLP2 enhanced arms also ran at a `2.2×` enhanced-to-unenhanced dose ratio (~+1484 enhanced rows on top of ~684 unenhanced) per [RLP2.md:55-58](../packets/RLP2.md), so dose and pool-composition were confounded.
- **2026-04-21 RLP3 lands the A1–A10 instrumentation stack** (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-21.md:56-304`). For gate-alignment purposes, three of the ten items are load-bearing:
  - **A6 carve-out**: enhanced-proper OOD lanes are monitored and flow into `value_composite` and `final_eval`, but **NOT into `ood_composite`**. Without this, `best_ood_composite` could be chosen to maximize enhanced-proper AUC, making "unenhanced-only training captures enhanced content" unfalsifiable. The carve-out is the gate-composition correction at instrument-level (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-21.md:517-525`, "A6 — enhanced-proper lanes carved out of `ood_composite`").
  - **A9 introduces `worst-pool-FPR` gate at `≤0.04`** (`:230-242, 527-535`). Critic-revision §8.4: *"§1.4 specifies 'minimum FPR across all real pools, equally weighted' — that reads as worst-case, not mean. The v3 formula used a pure mean, which allows one pool to be arbitrarily spiky."* This is the same gate-alignment principle that lands at scale in RLP6 (real-pool-only `worst_pool_fpr` correction); A9 imposes the worst-case ceiling on the FPR side at the metric level. If no τ satisfies both `mean_FPR(τ) == 0.02` AND `max_FPR(τ) ≤ 0.04`, `value_composite = NaN` with `value_composite_blocked_by = "worst_pool_fpr"`. The NaN is the signal that the model is hiding a spiky real pool.
  - **A10 90/10 video-id held-out partition** (`:276-303, 547-558`). Every OOD pool gets a `blake2b(video_id)` partition; 10% never logs during training. The bias estimate `auc_monitored_90 − auc_heldout_10` is the first direct measurement of selection bias per pool. The "A10 held-out bias audit" rule gates the entire packet's interpretation: if mean bias > σ_seed, AUC comparisons are compromised and must be re-read off held-out only.
- **2026-04-22 RLP3.5 gate-definition shift** (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_5_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-22.md:37-42`). Three knobs change globally: `target_mean_fpr 0.02 → 0.03`, `max_pool_fpr 0.04 → 0.05`, `stability_jitter_stat max → p95`. User-approved as deployment-defensible relaxation. The relaxation has visible effects on the value_composite stability term (sanity slot 02 stability `0.0008` under `max` vs `0.4475` under `p95` on the same checkpoint, `R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_RETRO_SCORE_RESULTS_2026-04-22.md:87` — the aggregator swap, not the threshold relaxation, is what unpinned the stability term). Gate-mass observation: under matched new gates, RLP3.5 leader `RLP35_02 (m=0.15)` clears `+0.0415` over retro-P3_02 but only `+0.021` over retro-P3 leader `RLP3_05 (low_arc + spatial)`. The "~5× smaller deltas" finding ([RLP3_5.md:62](../packets/RLP3_5.md)) is the first clean separation of *metric-moved-the-number* from *training-moved-the-number*. See [`promotion_contract_evolution`](promotion_contract_evolution.md) for the contract-side reading of the same evidence.
- **2026-04-22 first contract-side gate refusal in production** ([RLP3_5.md:52, 73](../packets/RLP3_5.md)). Slot `RLP35_03 (m=0.20)` sat at `worst_pool_fpr ≈ 0.055` for 22+ hours and never cleared the (relaxed) `0.05` ceiling. A checkpoint with otherwise-plausible training signal was unpromotable purely because a deployment-aligned gate refused to admit it. The pattern is the [gate_alignment_story](gate_alignment_story.md) at miniature scale; RLP4/RLP5 expand it; RLP6 reframes the entire gate (real-pool-only `worst_pool_fpr` + avspeech/VCD removal) and the ceiling jumps to 0.9006.

Four corrections converged during [RLP6](../packets/RLP6.md) planning:

- **`worst_pool_fpr` is real-pool-only.** Reading `trainer/trainer.py:161-216` showed `worst_pool_fpr` is computed exclusively over real pools. The prior framing that `wma_failure_fake` drove the gate was wrong by construction — a fake pool cannot drive a real-pool FPR metric. `docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET6_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-23.md:43-48` pins the correction.
- **`avspeech` is not deployment-distribution.** At natural τ, `external_youtube_avspeech_real` sits at 54.2% accuracy / 45.8% FPR. Keeping it in the gate force-pushes τ to ~0.995 to tame it, which crushes fake recall on the pools that do matter. Deployment target is Teams-passed reals, covered by `teams_ood_real` (97.8% / 2.2% FPR). Dropping avspeech + VCD from the real gate was predicted to recover ≥+0.10 on composite; slot-4 delivered +0.127 (0.7691 → 0.9006).
- **`visomaster_*` bad rows are weak-signal, not supervision.** The WT-A bad-data audit (`docs/VISOMASTER_BAD_DATA_POLICY_PLAN_IMPACT_2026-04-17.md:36-60`) classified 4,904 sample IDs as `ignore`, 480 as `visomaster hints`, 202 as `visomaster hints (teams)`. `teams_ood_fake` still carried hint folders mixed with clean fakes — the fake-side gate was grading the detector against near-real content labeled fake. `path_exclude_contains: ["/visomaster_"]` hygiene drops ~535 failed-deepfake hint folders from the gate. Predicted swing ≤ ±0.01; slot-2 corroborated.
- **`wma_failure_fake` drop is cleanup only.** Fréchet distance 14.55 from `deeplive_enh_fake` places it effectively in-distribution; it was detected at 99.4%. Not a `worst_pool_fpr` driver (real-pool-only), not a coverage hole. Slot-3 confirmed.

These four together reframed the ceiling. RLP5's `0.7736` ceiling was not representation-bound — it was gate-bound. The same backbone, same checkpoint, same data, scored against a corrected gate, jumps to ~0.90 without any retraining.

## Current stance (2026-04-24)

The three gate-alignment levers are settled and are the operating baseline for [RLP7](../packets/RLP7.md) and beyond:

1. **Avspeech and zoom_vcd are out of the deployment real gate permanently.** Not deployment-distribution; their FPR drove τ crazy. They can remain in a readout-only OOD block for observability (`R13_RLP6_06_avspeech_readout_only`).
2. **`path_exclude_contains: ["/visomaster_"]` on `teams_ood_fake` is standard.** Hint rows stay hint rows, not fake supervision. WT-A is the source of truth for which rows are hints.
3. **`wma_failure_fake` is demoted to readout-only.** Not a gate driver; kept for visibility.

Corrected `worst_pool_fpr` = real-pool-only is a permanent contract invariant. Fake-side pools cannot drive it; any future attempt to "fix `worst_pool_fpr` by editing a fake pool" is by construction a category error.

**Important constraint on the victory.** The gate-alignment lift is orthogonal and additive to the processing-signature shortcut. It does not touch the shortcut; RLP6_04 at `value_composite=0.9006` is the concrete slot that proves this (see [processing_signature_shortcut](processing_signature_shortcut.md)). Gate alignment was a real ~+0.13 lift; it did not and cannot close the camera/ISP problem. That is [RLP7](../packets/RLP7.md)'s job.

### Open loop: enhanced-vs-unenhanced-val-pool-confound
status: in-progress
severity: medium
first_seen: 2026-04-21
last_verified: 2026-04-29
close_criterion: a packet trains an enhanced-proper arm and measures it against a validation pool that explicitly contains enhanced-proper rows, with dose matched to the unenhanced arm — so "enhanced hurts" / "enhanced helps" is readable without the pool-composition + dose confound

RLP2 (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-21.md:24-29`) called *"enhanced hurts"* in-session on `RLP2_03` and `RLP2_04` against `RLP2_02`, but RLP3's planning doc downgraded that to muddled because (1) `val_holdout` for `RLP2_02` had zero enhanced-proper content (the val pool was unenhanced-only), and (2) the enhanced arms ran at `~2.2×` the unenhanced dose (~1484 vs ~684 enhanced fake rows; [RLP2.md:55-58](../packets/RLP2.md)). Two confounds, the same direction, no separability. RLP3 (Slice 3) addresses this by carving `A6 enhanced-proper OOD lanes for every run` plus a dose-matched slot 08 — those two interventions, taken together, are the close-criterion observable. The loop is `in-progress` because the RLP3 instruments exist on paper at end of Slice 2; the readout that finally separates the components belongs to Slice 3.

**Slice 3 verification (2026-04-29).** RLP3 landed both instruments as planned: A6 carve-out is in `data/sources/combined_paired.py:1808-2000` (extended `_build_external_ood_videos()` reads enhanced lanes from the proper-data manifest) and A6 yaml plumbing is uniform across all 8 RLP3 slots. Slot 08 (`R13_RLP3_08_FT_proper_main_plus_dose_matched_enhanced.yaml`) added `proper_visomaster_enhanced_teams` at ~342 rows (1:1 with unenhanced teams dose). However, the RLP3 retro-score results doc (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_RETRO_SCORE_RESULTS_2026-04-22.md`) reports retro NEW-gate value_composite for slots 02/04/05 only ([RLP3.md:48](../packets/RLP3.md): *"Slot 07 lighting and slot 08 dose-matched enhanced: non-promoting in-session (07 failed ≥2/3 lighting-preset threshold; 08 did not beat 02 on enhanced-proper by more than σ_seed)"*). So slot 08's non-promotion is recorded at the in-session level, but the retro-score table covering 08's enhanced-proper readouts vs. slot 02's enhanced-proper readouts (the actual close-criterion observable) was never explicitly produced in Slice 3 sources. Two of the three confounds (pool composition, dose) are addressed structurally; the third (split-mode flip from `shuffle → hash_stable`, see loop `split-mode-delta-unquantified`) is **not** addressed by RLP3 and remains unbounded. Loop stays `in-progress` because slot 08's negative-result is informative but the precise enhanced-vs-unenhanced separation was not surfaced as a numeric readout, and split-mode is still confounded with everything that crosses the RLP1↔RLP2+ boundary.

**Slice 4 verification (2026-04-29).** RLP5's E3 recipe (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_VISOMASTER_TEAMS_POOL_DIAGNOSTIC_FINDINGS_2026-04-23.md:186-226`) directly addresses the lane-composition half of the original confound: enabling `proper_visomaster_enhanced_teams` in `include_lanes` + bumping `proper_visomaster_teams_fake` family weight 1.0 → 2.5 closed the per-frame fake-side accuracy gap from 67%/18% → 94%/96% on the slot-07 checkpoint (`analysis/feature_space_2026-04-23/per_source_summary.csv:12-15`). This is the first slice where an RLP run trains on enhanced-teams AND measures against a pool that contains it — so "enhanced helps" is now readable on the training-side composite. RLP5_07 `best_value_composite=0.7736` is the magnitude. **However** — and this is why the loop does not move to `resolved` — the Slice-4 evidence carries two new caveats stacked on top of the resolution: (1) the slot-07 checkpoint is preprocessing-pre-fix (commit `855871e` lands later in the slice), so the 94%/96% headline numbers are pre-fix retro-score values; only RLP6_04's anchor pool has been re-scored post-fix. See [`preprocessing_parity_bug`](preprocessing_parity_bug.md). (2) The slot-07 checkpoint that closed the gap was simultaneously found to encode a camera/ISP-signature shortcut (memory `project_signature_shortcut_finding.md`; same person `dor_shkedi` vs `real_dor` flips model output), so the "94%/96% closure" is partly shortcut-driven. The training-side gate-composition fix is real; the deployment-side interpretation is shortcut-entangled. Loop stays `in-progress` rather than `resolved`: the close criterion ("dose matched, pool composition fixed, split-mode quantified") is now `2 of 3` confounds addressed; split-mode and the post-fix re-score of the enhanced lift remain open.
