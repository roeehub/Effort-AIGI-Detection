## Section 2 — Lens 2: per-substrate τ-calibration lift

Source: calibration_lift_per_ckpt.csv (script run_audit.py::lens2_calibration_lift).

For each ckpt × fake suite × FPR target ∈ {0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20}:
- τ_dev_cal = quantile(teams_real_all_dev scores, 1−FPR)
- τ_lockbox_cal = quantile(teams_real_all_lockbox scores, 1−FPR)
- recall_dev_cal = mean(fake_scores > τ_dev_cal)
- recall_lockbox_cal = mean(fake_scores > τ_lockbox_cal)
- lift = recall_lockbox_cal − recall_dev_cal

## Section 3 — Lens 3: image-quality shortcut on viso

Source: iq_shortcut_per_ckpt.csv (script run_audit.py::lens3_iq_shortcut).

Method: 5-fold StratifiedKFold L1-logistic regression (C=0.5, solver=liblinear,
max_iter=1000, n_jobs=1, random_state=42) on standardized 7 IQ features
(luma_p10, luma_p90, luma_mean, laplacian_var, sobel_edge_mean, saturation_mean,
skin_frac) predicting per-ckpt is_caught (= score > τ_dev_cal at FPR=10%).
N=550 viso fakes.

| ckpt | n_caught | majority baseline | CV acc mean ± std | CV AUC mean ± std | top-1 feature (coef) | top-2 feature (coef) | top-3 feature (coef) |
|------|---------:|------------------:|-------------------|--------------------|----------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| P8A | 148 | 0.7309 | 0.7909 ± 0.0230 | 0.7474 ± 0.0340 | saturation_mean (−0.755) | luma_p90 (−0.513) | sobel_edge_mean (+0.481) |
| E2B_3200 | 46 | 0.9164 | 0.9145 ± 0.0073 | 0.8665 ± 0.0498 | sobel_edge_mean (−1.656) | luma_mean (+1.100) | luma_p10 (−0.701) |
| E3_6600 | 76 | 0.8618 | 0.8673 ± 0.0073 | 0.7651 ± 0.0379 | luma_p10 (−1.182) | saturation_mean (−0.603) | sobel_edge_mean (−0.448) |

Notes:
- CV acc relative to majority baseline: P8A +5.99pp, E2B_3200 −0.18pp, E3_6600 +0.55pp.
- CV AUC: P8A 0.7474, E2B_3200 0.8665, E3_6600 0.7651.
- All three top-1 features differ in sign across ckpts.
- E2B_3200 and E3_6600 have heavy class-imbalance (only 46 / 76 caught of 550), which inflates majority baseline.

### Coverage limitation

| P8A | deeplive | 0.8614 | 0.4239 | 0.9982 | 43.76 |

| P8A | teams_fake_lockbox | 0.8982 | 0.5412 | 0.9953 | 35.70 |
| E2B_3200 | viso | 0.6815 | 0.0836 | 0.9000 | 59.79 |
| E2B_3200 | deeplive | 0.9654 | 0.9394 | 1.0000 | 2.59 |
| E2B_3200 | teams_fake_dev | 0.9261 | 0.7940 | 0.9819 | 13.21 |
| E2B_3200 | teams_fake_lockbox | 0.9479 | 0.8306 | 1.0000 | 11.74 |
| E3_6600 | viso | 0.7404 | 0.1382 | 0.9109 | 60.22 |
| E3_6600 | deeplive | 0.9547 | 0.9083 | 1.0000 | 4.64 |
| E3_6600 | teams_fake_dev | 0.9229 | 0.7989 | 0.9839 | 12.40 |
| E3_6600 | teams_fake_lockbox | 0.9653 | 0.9435 | 1.0000 | 2.18 |

Notes:
- Largest gaps live on viso (48–60 pp across ckpts).
- Smallest gap: E3_6600 / teams_fake_lockbox = 2.18 pp.
- Each ckpt has a different smallest-gap suite: P8A → teams_fake_dev (21.17),
  E2B_3200 → deeplive (2.59), E3_6600 → teams_fake_lockbox (2.18).
- recall@FPR=50% reaches ≥0.86 on every (ckpt × suite) cell.

gap is a heuristic — it mixes scales (AUC is rank-based, recall is point-based)
and a small gap can reflect saturation rather than threshold-tuning headroom. It
is recorded as a quick read; do not treat it as a calibrated discrimination
metric.
