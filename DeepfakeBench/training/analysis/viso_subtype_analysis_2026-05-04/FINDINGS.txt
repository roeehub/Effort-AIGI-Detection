project_lockbox_fpr_dominated_by_webcam_mode.md). The teams transport has done to
viso fakes what webcam capture did to lockbox reals: shoved them into the model's
"hard / blurry / bright / desaturated" domain.

------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT (HIGH CONFIDENCE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Viso->teams is hard PRIMARILY because the transport pushes image quality into a
region the model already fails on, regardless of method.

Three converging lines:
  1. Transport shifts luma/sharpness/saturation by Cohen's d = 0.5..5.2, in the
     SAME DIRECTION as the previously-documented webcam-mode failure axis.
  2. Within each subtype, IQ-only L1 logistic predicts catch with 72-76% CV acc
     -- IQ retains predictive grip in both halves; no hidden transport axis.
  3. Sharpness lever FLIPS OFF in teams (d=+0.80 -> d=-0.11) -- entire teams
     subtype dragged below the discrimination band, exactly the pattern when a
     domain shift saturates a feature out of usable range.

Residual uncertainty: cross-ckpt correlation collapse in teams (P8A<->E2B
Spearman 0.745 -> 0.099) leaves ~25% of teams uncaught variance unexplained by
IQ alone. There IS some axis on which architectures disagree about teams frames.
But that is SECONDARY on top of the primary IQ-compounded story.

  Confidence:   HIGH on the IQ-compounded explanation.
                MODERATE that there is a small additional transport-specific
                  component in the residual.

------------------------------------------------------------
IMPLICATIONS FOR NEXT-PACKET DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Don't aim a packet at "transport signature." The hard part is image-quality
   robustness. A packet whose only lever is transport-aware augmentation will
   probably fail like P14_DATA_FIX did -- binding constraint is tau-tail
   separation under blur/brightness shift, not data presence.

2. The right lever is sharpness/brightness invariance. P22's pipeline_randomization
   is the closest existing match: it traded train AUC for 3x dev fake macro recall
   and dropped Pearson |r| 0.44 -> 0.12 (memory: project_p22_succeeded_2026-05-02
   and the step1k reframe). A P22-derived packet that EXPLICITLY hits gaussian-blur
   + brightness-jitter at training time is the natural next move.

3. P8A and E2B catch DIFFERENT teams frames (Spearman 0.099). On this 275-frame
   teams subset, the union of P8A (49) and E2B (36) substantially overlaps with
   the union-of-three (67). E3 contributes very little marginal teams catch.
   A P8A + E2B (B16 scratch+CE) ensemble on teams-style frames is essentially free.

4. Add a viso-teams-specific eval slice. The 19 pp transport gap is large enough
   that it should be a first-class scorecard slice rather than buried inside
   visomaster_enhanced_macro_dev. The promotion contract currently aggregates
   across a 56.7% / 75.6% split that hides which subtype a candidate lifts.

5. Don't reach for arch changes again. L14 (E3) and B16-scratch (E2B) don't help
   the teams subtype either -- three architecturally distinct ckpts, all failing.
   The blocker is upstream of architecture.
