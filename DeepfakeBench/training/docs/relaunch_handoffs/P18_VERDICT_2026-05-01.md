# P18 verdict — γ (no bite)  ⚠ PARTIALLY INVALIDATED — see correction below ⚠

> **CORRECTION (2026-05-02)**: The "γ no bite" verdict in this document was based on a probe (`extract_p18_features.py`'s 12-class macro-OVR AUC) that could NOT have detected biting even if biting occurred. The probe averaged inter-bucket separation across 3 trivially-separable content categories present in the eval substrate, while the actual Phase 1A shortcut axis is INTRA-bucket-3 (dor_shkedi-style identities vs other Teams identities). A corrective probe (`corrective_probes.py`) found a Δ=−0.043 within-bucket-3 LR AUC (treatment 0.9536 vs control 0.9965) and Δ=−0.52 lockbox dor-real FPR @ τ=0.5 (treatment 40% vs control 92%) — directionally consistent with β partial bite, BUT at n_dor=9 / n_lockbox_dor=25 the result is statistically fragile. Honest current verdict: **inconclusive pending more diagnostics**. See [`HANDOFF_2026-05-02_P18_CORRECTIVE.md`](HANDOFF_2026-05-02_P18_CORRECTIVE.md) for the full picture, methodological caveats, and a concrete diagnostics-needed list. The remainder of this document is preserved for the historical record but its core conclusion should NOT be cited without the correction.

## TL;DR

12-class method-conditional GRL with λ ramping to 0.95 produced an encoder essentially indistinguishable from the no-GRL control on its target axis. The encoder-side 12-class linear probe at the FINAL ckpts: treatment macro-OVR AUC = **0.9954**, control = **0.9955**, P8A baseline = 0.998. The GRL pressure left no discernible mark on the encoder's [CLS] manifold's discriminability of the 12 method buckets — including `deeplive_enhanced` (Phase 1A's flagged axis), where AUC remained at 0.9999.

This is the **third consecutive single-axis GRL/lever failure** at the architectural level: P15 GRL @ static λ=0.20 (capture-mode axis, didn't bite), P14 bundle (FT-from-scratch, recipe-driven, didn't promote), and P18 ramped GRL @ λ→0.95 on the Phase-1A-validated method axis (this verdict). The architectural-lever space has been exhausted for FT-from-P8A workflows.

The destructor identified by Phase 1A — the encoder's strong discriminability of identity/fake-method clusters — is **not removable by adversarial gradient on its own**. Either the gradient cannot reach the encoder paths that encode the cluster (the SVD-residual unfreeze recipe routes most parameter updates through narrow-rank residuals that may absorb GRL pressure), or the cluster is so dominant in the training distribution's class signal that no λ schedule short of catastrophic destruction will erase it.

**Direct implication: pivot to Move 4 paired same-identity contrastive** (Option II in `PHASE1_SYNTHESIS_2026-05-01.md`). By construction, paired training forbids identity as a shortcut (anchor + positive = same identity, opposite labels), so the encoder cannot learn to separate fake-method clusters using identity-correlated features. ~2–3 days of new code (paired sampler + contrastive loss head + trainer forward-pass changes), then ~$60 Vertex.

## Encoder-side probe (load-bearing)

This is the canonical "did GRL bite at the encoder's [CLS] manifold?" test. Re-extracts features with each arm's LIVE encoder on the 800-frame eval substrate, then fits a 12-class one-vs-rest LR on the 3 active method-domain buckets (the eval substrate has only 3: `deeplive_enhanced`=2, `deeplive_teams`=3, `realpool_real`=11; per Phase 3.5 smoke gate).

| Source | Macro-OVR AUC | bucket 2 (deeplive_enhanced) | bucket 3 (deeplive_teams) | bucket 11 (realpool_real) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P8A baseline (Phase 3.5 smoke gate) | **0.998** | 0.9999 | 0.9973 | 0.9969 |
| Control (`rgt4kw2u`) step4000 | **0.9955** | 0.9998 | 0.9939 | 0.9929 |
| Treatment (`xpbvc1e4`) step4000 | **0.9954** | 0.9999 | 0.9927 | 0.9934 |

The **treatment vs control delta is essentially zero** on every bucket. The 0.0001 macro-OVR delta is well within sampling noise. The encoder did not become more substrate-invariant under GRL pressure than under no GRL.

`bucket 2 (deeplive_enhanced)` is the bucket Phase 1A specifically pinpointed as the trained P17 head's modal alignment axis. Per-bucket AUC went 0.9999 (P8A) → 0.9999 (treatment) — completely unchanged. GRL on bucket 2 produced zero flattening of bucket-2 discriminability.

Two notable patterns:
1. **Both arms pulled their head direction off the dor_shkedi axis** as training progressed (treatment to -0.153, control even further to -0.279). This is consistent with GRL pressure rotating the head off the natural "predict fake/real on dev" direction without finding a better deployment-grade alternative.

## Why this is a cleaner γ than P15's γ

P15 GRL @ static λ=0.20 (run `w5tky6ss`) failed to bite at the [CLS] manifold (domain-confusion probe AUC 0.9994). Phase 1A explained why: the GRL was reversing a gradient on the *capture-mode* axis the encoder wasn't using.

P18 took Phase 1A's diagnostic and built a 12-bucket method-conditional GRL specifically targeting the axes the encoder *was* using (via the audit at `analysis/method_class_audit_2026-05-01/proposed_method_domain_map.py`). Pre-launch smoke gate confirmed P8A's encoder linearly discriminated those 12 buckets at macro-OVR AUC 0.998 — strong signal for GRL to attack.

**P18 is the first GRL run aimed at the empirically-correct axis.** It still didn't bite. That decisively rules out "GRL on the right axis" as the architectural lever, *for the FT-from-P8A workflow on the SVD-residual unfreeze recipe*.

What's NOT yet ruled out:
- GRL with a different *unfreeze recipe* (full backbone fine-tune, no SVD residual constraints). Would require new training run; speculative.
- GRL with a fundamentally different gradient surface (e.g., REx — invariant risk minimization — or domain-adversarial via mutual-information bounds). Speculative; new code.
- **Move 4 paired same-identity contrastive**: structurally different — directly enforces invariance via training data construction, not gradient reversal. The proper-data wave (705 paired identities) is already in hand; companion_bucket plumbing already exists.

## Decision and next packet recommendation

**Skip the promotion-contract scorecard on P18.** Phase 3.7's P8A v3-substrate analog already established that P8A doesn't pass any contract floor on production-tight crops (NOT tier-0 under either 0.70 or 0.30). Treatment's val AUC = 0.9907 is essentially the same as P8A's — no reason to expect different scorecard outcome. Spending ~$20 to re-confirm "FT-from-P8A doesn't promote" on top of (a) mclioexb non-promotion and (b) the P8A v3-substrate analog is wasted budget.

**Recommended Phase 5 packet — Move 4 paired same-identity contrastive (Option II from `PHASE1_SYNTHESIS_2026-05-01.md`):**

1. **Paired sampler** in `combined_paired.py` — integrate `companion_bucket` plumbing for `_load_merged_teams_enhanced_paired`. Each batch: anchor (real frame from identity X) + positive (real frame from identity X under different substrate / different recapture) + fake (swap of identity X). Currently the sampler emits clean+swapped pairs but doesn't pin them to the same identity — Move 4 makes that explicit and adversarial.

2. **Contrastive loss head** on `effort_detector.py` — exposes a contrastive embedding (e.g., 128-dim projection of [CLS]) and applies SupCon or NT-Xent loss with the supervision signal: same-identity-different-substrate pairs are pulled together; different-identity-same-substrate pairs are pushed apart. Existing `loss/contrastive_regularization.py:39` `ContrastiveLoss` is for UCF-style disentanglement and cannot be used as-is — need a new effort-compatible variant.

3. **Trainer forward-pass changes** — collate fn must construct positive pairs at batch time; loss aggregation must combine CE + contrastive.

4. **Pre-launch CPU smoke gate** (analog of Phase 3.5): confirm same-identity pairs from proper-data are loadable + the contrastive forward pass produces finite loss + gradients hit the encoder. Estimated: ½ day.

5. **Vertex launch** — single packet (no control needed since contrastive is structurally different from baseline; control = no-contrastive runs already exist in the form of P14_FT_FROM_P8A or P18 control). ~$60–$120 / 8h.

**Estimated total**: 2–3 days new code + smoke + ~$60–$120 Vertex. The proper-data wave (705 paired identities) is already cached in GCS.

If Move 4 also doesn't bite, the next pivot is **production-tight retraining** (deterministic RFA=0.85 crops at training time; ~1 day new bbox-aware crop transform + retrain). That addresses Move 1.5's load-bearing finding (eval-FPR underestimates production-FPR by 12+ pp).
