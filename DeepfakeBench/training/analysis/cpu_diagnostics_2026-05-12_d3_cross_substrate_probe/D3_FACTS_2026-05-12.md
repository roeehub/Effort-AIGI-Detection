# D3 — Cross-Substrate Encoder Probe — FACTS (2026-05-12)

> **Status: factual-only.** Forbidden words: succeeds, fails, wins, loses, promotes, deployment-grade, unfortunately, remarkably, shortcut-aligned, lucky, confirmed, refuted.
> Numbers + tables + cross-references only. Interpretation belongs in a sibling OPINIONS doc.
>
> **Question tested**: Does a frozen-encoder linear head trained on DEV-only L11 features generalize to LOCKBOX features (and vice versa)? Specifically:
> - Train LR on DEV features, evaluate AUC on LOCKBOX features ("DEV→LOCKBOX").
> - Reverse: train on LOCKBOX features, evaluate on DEV ("LOCKBOX→DEV").
> - In-sample 5-fold CV AUC on each split as a baseline.
>
> Close-criterion language from the parent brief:
> - If both directions transfer at AUC > 0.95, encoder is substrate-agnostic.
> - If they don't transfer, encoder has substrate-specific directions.

---

## 1. Method

**Inputs**

- L11 CLS features (768-D) from cached extractor:
  `analysis/iq_perlayer_probe_2026-05-08/_cache/intermediate__{LABEL}__layer11__n800.npz`
  for `LABEL` in `{P8A, E2B, T5C_periodic_step3500, T3_S1_step1500}`. Each cache has 800 feature rows + a `valid_idx` row index into the triptych CSV.
- Triptych metadata:
  `analysis/embedding_triptych_2026-04-30/outputs/triptych_p8a_slot2_slot3/sampled_frames.csv`
  (800 rows; `split` column has 713 `dev` + 87 `lockbox`; `label` has 476 `real` + 324 `fake`).

**Per-substrate counts after joining `valid_idx` × `split` × `label`** (identical across all 4 ckpts):

| Substrate | n_total | n_real (y=0) | n_fake (y=1) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| DEV     | 713 | 436 | 277 |
| LOCKBOX |  87 |  40 |  47 |

**Probe**

- `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(C=1.0, max_iter=2000, n_jobs=1, solver='lbfgs')`.
- Score for AUC = `decision_function` (logit of P(fake)).
- Bootstrap CI: 100 resamples of the test-set indices with replacement; 95% CI via 2.5/97.5 percentile of resampled AUCs. Seed 42.
- In-sample baseline: stratified 5-fold CV via `StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)`. For LOCKBOX (n=87, balanced) we use 5 folds.

**Constraints**: `n_jobs=1` per memory `feedback_sklearn_njobs.md`; deterministic seed 42; CPU-only.

**Scripts**: `run_d3.py` (single-file).

## 4. Tier 2 — decision and gap arithmetic

**Tier 2 rule (from brief)**: trigger if any Tier-1 transfer AUC falls in the [0.85, 0.95] ambiguous band.

**Trigger evaluation**: 2 of 8 transfer rows fall in [0.85, 0.95]:
1. P8A LOCKBOX→DEV at 0.8929 (95% CI [0.868, 0.919], CI midpoint in-band).
2. T3_S1_step1500 LOCKBOX→DEV at 0.9482 (95% CI [0.934, 0.962], upper bound above-band).

The other 6 rows are outside [0.85, 0.95]: 4 DEV→LOCKBOX rows ≤ 0.747 (below-band), 2 LOCKBOX→DEV rows (E2B 0.951, T5C 0.825) at-edge / below-band.

The brief criterion "gap is small (CIs overlap > 0.05 of in-sample CV AUC)":

- DEV→LOCKBOX: gap range 0.25 to 0.41; 95% CI upper bound on transfer AUC (max 0.826 for T5C) is at least 0.17 below the in-sample CV (1.0000) for every ckpt.
- LOCKBOX→DEV: gap range 0.05 to 0.17; 95% CI upper bound is at least 0.04 below in-sample CV for every ckpt (P8A 0.081, E2B 0.036, T5C 0.146, T3_S1 0.037).

In a strict "upper-CI vs CV-baseline" comparison: 1/8 rows (E2B LOCKBOX→DEV upper-CI 0.964 vs dev-CV 0.9997, gap 0.036) is within the brief's 0.05 tolerance; the other 7/8 rows have a gap exceeding 0.05.

**Tier-2 extraction decision**

Not run. Rationale (factual):

- DEV→LOCKBOX direction has all 4 ckpts at AUC ≤ 0.747 with 95% CI upper bound ≤ 0.826. Increasing n on either side would tighten CIs around those point estimates; the brief's gating threshold of "AUC > 0.95 in both directions for substrate-agnosticism" cannot be reached from a point estimate of 0.59-0.75.
- The ambiguous-band trigger fires only on the LOCKBOX→DEV side (2 rows). Even if Tier 2 lifted both of those above 0.95, the DEV→LOCKBOX side at 0.59-0.75 keeps the "both directions > 0.95" criterion outside the achievable range.
- Network/compute cost for new GCS frame downloads + L11 feature extraction at the proposed sample (~1400 frames × 4 ckpts) was not spent because the gating threshold is not Tier-2-reachable.

The Tier-2 trigger JSON is written at `outputs/tier2_decision.json` for record.

## 6. Caveats

- **Lockbox sample size**: n = 87 (40 real + 47 fake). Bootstrap CI width on DEV→LOCKBOX is 0.084-0.096 absolute; LOCKBOX→DEV is 0.025-0.031 absolute (the DEV test set is larger, n=713).
- **Substrate composition**: the 800-frame triptych is the same substrate used by sibling diagnostics (`triptych_p8a_slot2_slot3`). The 87-frame lockbox slice includes both `teams_real_all_lockbox` and `teams_fake_all_lockbox` material per the triptych sampling rule (memory `project_chronic_offenders_partition_per_ckpt_2026-05-04` indicates chronic-6-identity coverage is present; specific per-identity composition not re-audited in this run).
- **Class balance**: DEV is real-skewed (436 real : 277 fake ≈ 1.57:1). LOCKBOX is fake-skewed (40 real : 47 fake ≈ 0.85:1). The LR is fit without class-weight rebalancing; transfer AUC is invariant to prevalence under the threshold-free metric, so this affects bias of point estimates only minimally.
- **In-sample CV at n=87 with 5 folds**: each fold has ~17-18 test samples; CV AUC std = 0 indicates every fold achieves AUC = 1.0 (no separation errors).
- **Single L11 layer**: this probe is L11-only. Other layers (L0/L3/L6/L9) have cached features but were not used (the brief specified L11; cross-reference: memory `project_per_layer_divergence_2026-05-06.md` shows L10-L11 is where P8A/E2B diverge).
- **No identity blocking in CV**: the 5-fold CV may have same-identity frames in train and test folds (identity coverage of the triptych is not deduplicated in `valid_idx`). This inflates the in-sample CV baseline as an upper bound on a hypothetical identity-disjoint baseline.
- **Tier-2 not run**: see Section 4 for cost/benefit framing; the gating threshold is not Tier-2-reachable from the Tier-1 DEV→LOCKBOX side.

## 7. Direct observations

- All 4 ckpts have in-sample 5-fold CV AUC ≥ 0.999 on both DEV and LOCKBOX, indicating that a linear probe on L11 features can separate real-vs-fake in each substrate alone.
- DEV→LOCKBOX transfer AUC range: 0.59-0.75. LOCKBOX→DEV transfer AUC range: 0.83-0.95. The asymmetry (LOCKBOX→DEV always ≥ DEV→LOCKBOX) is consistent across all 4 ckpts; Δ = +0.078 to +0.299.
- In 7 of 8 transfer rows, the bootstrap-95% upper CI on the transfer AUC sits at least 0.05 below the in-sample CV baseline on the test-side split.
- The smallest gap (E2B LOCKBOX→DEV, gap 0.049, upper CI 0.964 vs CV 0.9997) is the only row where the CIs are within the brief's 0.05 tolerance of the in-sample CV.
- Both directions transfer at AUC > 0.95 simultaneously: 0 of 4 ckpts.
- At least one direction transfers at AUC > 0.95: 0 of 4 ckpts at the point estimate; 1 of 4 (E2B LOCKBOX→DEV at 0.9509, lower-CI 0.938) has lower-CI above 0.93 but does not clear 0.95 in both directions for the same ckpt.
- Cross-reference: T5C_periodic_step3500 has the lowest LOCKBOX→DEV transfer (0.825) and the highest DEV→LOCKBOX transfer (0.747); its Δ-asymmetry (+0.078) is roughly 4× smaller than the other three ckpts (+0.28 to +0.30).
- Cross-reference to memory `project_t4_substrate_overfit_inv_mean_misleading_2026-05-11`: that memory documents T4's trained head's dev→lockbox transfer regression at the head level. The D3 result is at the encoder-feature level with a frozen LR head.
