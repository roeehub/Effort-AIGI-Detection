## Conclusions drawn in-session

1. **The drop-list lever (T3_SLOT1) is the only T3 lever that produced a floor-passing candidate.** Slots 2 (IQ-matched DF40 pairs) and 3 (per-method IQ-matched pairs) both fail dev recall floor at all auto-calibrated τ.
2. **F0 contract policy under-reads T3's capability on the F4 / HDTF substrates that actually match the user's deployment policy.** Under F0+chronic-6, step2500's auto-τ clips at 0.971 to control real-FPR, which kills dev fake recall as an artifact. Under F4 (chronic-6 dropped), the same model hits 79% viso / 100% deeplive / 96% teams_fake at 10% FPR.
3. **The Mac probe's deployment-τ FPR (1% on dor lockbox at τ=0.92, matches P8A) does NOT predict the contract scorecard reading at auto-calibrated τ=0.682** — operating points matter; the same model has different binding metrics at different thresholds. This is broader than the existing memory `train_auc_not_valid_promotion_signal` — it applies to any cohort-specific Mac probe.
4. **Step2500 dominates step1500 on F4 v2 + HDTF teams**, only loses to it on the F0 v2 dev recall floor. Choice between the two is a calibration-policy choice, not a capability choice.
5. **Roy_D regression is the universal obstacle**, present in all 5 scored T3 ckpts. Mechanism appears to be encoder-level shift away from color/saturation as real-anchor signals — supports a hard-negative-mining or face_scale_jitter T4.
6. **The "viso 27% production ceiling" framing was always-already conditional on F0 substrate.** T3_SLOT1's F4 viso 73-79% breaks the F4 ceiling cleanly; on F0 the ceiling holds because the auto-τ artifact dominates.

## Retrospective

### What went right

- **Single-lever discipline maintained.** Each of 3 slots tested ONE thing on top of a common base. The scorecard verdict is interpretable per-slot.
- **Measurement infrastructure was already in place.** F4 substrate-cleaning eval (built 2026-05-05 for PA), Mac dor cohort + Roy_D probe (built for Stage 2), promotion-contract scorecard launcher (existed since P14). No new infra blocked progress.
- **Mac probe gave a fast pre-Vertex pulse** that flagged step2500 + step1000 as interesting, even though the eventual F0 contract-scorecard verdict was different.
- **HDTF cross-substrate test caught the v2-vs-substrate-bound question early** — without it, we would have a step2500 F4 win unverified.

### What went wrong

- **Initial framing of "T3 fails Pillar 2" was wrong.** Mac probe scores at τ=0.5 looked catastrophic (dor lockbox 60-80% FPR); user pushed back ("0.190 isn't actually 10× worse than 0.019"); deployment-τ analysis at τ=0.92 revealed Mac-probe 1% match with P8A. The lesson is: **always view chronic-cluster-FPR through the deployment-τ lens, not through score-multiplier**.
- **The "data axis pulled twice no lift" memory was overstated** even at writing — and is now further refuted. Memory `project_data_axis_lever_pulled_twice_no_lift.md` should be amended to "pulled four times; PA + T3_SLOT1 lifted F4 viso substantively; the F0 ceiling persists due to chronic-6 calibration artifact, not data-volume".
- **Initial morning brief framing as "step1500 is the SOTA candidate" was overturned 3 hours later** when step2500 HDTF result landed. step2500 is the actual SOTA. The morning brief's TL;DR was revised before the user woke.
- **Two HDTF Vertex jobs both `JOB_STATE_FAILED` at the contract-scoring tail** — same known bug as 2026-05-05 P8A-on-HDTF. Per-suite reports completed cleanly so the failure is non-blocking. The bug is in `score_teams_promotion_contract.py:748` (hardcoded `--dev_real_suite teams_real_all_dev` not present in the HDTF manifest); fixing it would need a second-pass commit on the runner.

### Surprises

- **Step2500 generalizes better to HDTF than step1500.** Naively, step2500 should be MORE v2-substrate-overfit than step1500 (longer training on the v2 distribution). Empirically it's not — step2500 has +7.6pp on `proper_visomaster_enhanced_teams_dev` vs step1500. This suggests the lever's effect is encoder-level, not memorization-level.
- **T3_SLOT1 is ALSO better than P8A on HDTF clean transport** (`proper_visomaster_clean_dev` 100% vs P8A 98.9%, `proper_visomaster_enhanced_clean_dev` 99.7% vs 98.6%). The lever didn't only buy v2 lift — it actually added some HDTF-clean-substrate capability too.
- **Score-IQ correlation analysis pinpointed the mechanism**: T3 weakened color_a_dev / saturation as real-discrimination signals (P8A ρ=-0.59 → T3 ρ=-0.45) and added luma_mean (P8A +0.22 → T3 +0.49). The same shift that helps non-chronic reals (more diverse signal) breaks Roy_D (whose color_a anchor is no longer used).
