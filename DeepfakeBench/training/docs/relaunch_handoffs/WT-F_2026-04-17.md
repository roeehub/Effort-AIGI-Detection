## Key Decisions

- Future proper data uses only explicit `proper_*` exact lanes.
- The six canonical exact lanes are:
  - `proper_real_clean`
  - `proper_real_teams`
  - `proper_visomaster_clean`
  - `proper_visomaster_enhanced_clean`
  - `proper_visomaster_teams`
  - `proper_visomaster_enhanced_teams`
- Clean and Teams variants stay explicit and parallel through `base_capture_id`.
- Split hygiene is driven by `split_group_id` before variant expansion.
- Future proper data must not be folded into:
  - `visomaster hints`
  - `visomaster hints (teams)`
  - `visomaster_teams_enhanced`
