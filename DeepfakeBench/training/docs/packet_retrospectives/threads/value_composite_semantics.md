# Thread: `value_composite` semantics — deployment-grade or training-side directional?

## The question

`value_composite` was introduced in [RLP3](../packets/RLP3.md) as A9: a weighted in-trainer composite (`0.6 · teams_fakes_tpr + 0.3 · other_fakes_tpr + 0.1 · stability` at the τ meeting `mean_FPR ≤ 0.02 AND max_FPR ≤ 0.04` across 6 real pools). It selects the checkpoint within a packet (`best_value_composite` is the canonical save). Through Slice 4 the team treated it loosely — "the trainer's directional selection metric, the contract is the deployment readout" — without naming the operational rule cleanly. Slice 5 forces the question: when can `value_composite` legitimately be quoted as a deployment-relevant claim, and when is it provably blind to the failure mode under discussion?

## Initial belief

Through [RLP3](../packets/RLP3.md), [RLP3.5](../packets/RLP3_5.md), and [RLP4](../packets/RLP4.md), `value_composite` was used as the canonical readout — "RLP3 leader's `value_composite=0.7232`", "RLP3.5 leader at `0.7442`", "RLP6_04 at `0.9006`". The June-17 plan's three-layer split (training selection / contract readout / lockbox gate) was implicit but never explicit at the metric level. Composite numbers were quoted in handoffs without always disclosing whether they were trainer-side, contract-side, or lockbox-side; and the "trainer composite is directional, not deployment-grade" rule lived in `project_promotion_contract.md` and `project_signature_shortcut_finding.md` rather than in any per-yaml or per-packet contract artifact.

## What changed our mind

- **2026-04-24 [RLP6](../packets/RLP6.md) slot-04 with `value_composite=0.9006` is the first concrete proof that the metric is deployment-blind to the camera/ISP shortcut.** The 2-camera test (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET7_CAMERA_SIGNATURE_HANDOFF_2026-04-24.md:5-19`) flips Dor on different cameras (laptop 0.02 vs webcam 0.94). At τ catching 90% of fakes, real FPR is 63.6% on the same RLP6_04 checkpoint per memory `project_signature_shortcut_finding.md`. The composite gate's real pools do not cover the failure surface, so the deployment block is invisible at the trainer composite level. Across Slice 4 this hardens [`promotion_contract_evolution`](promotion_contract_evolution.md)'s stance from "lockbox is the authority of record" to "lockbox is the hard gate; the composite is informational".
- **2026-04-25 R13 full-story doc explicitly demotes `value_composite` to non-deployment-grade** (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_FULL_STORY_PRE_PACKET_9_2026-04-25.md:111`). Verbatim: *"Why the trainer's `value_composite` is not deployment-grade: it is computed against a fixed snapshot of dev data and uses heuristic thresholds (target_mean_fpr 0.03, max_pool_fpr 0.05). It does not reflect the lockbox distribution and does not perform the lexicographic τ search. A run can score `value_composite ≈ 0.99` and still fail the contract — exactly what is happening with P8A right now."* The "exactly what is happening" sentence dates the operational shift: P8A's W&B summary value_composite is 0.99+ at step 5000, but the lockbox-anchored promotion contract scorecard is the only readout that surfaces the per-method fake-recall regression.
- **2026-04-25 P8A regression analysis makes the failure-mode legible** (`R13_FULL_STORY_PRE_PACKET_9_2026-04-25.md:163-205`). 329 fake-side videos where RLP6_04 catches the fake but P8A misses, with **zero compensating gains**. The trainer's `value_composite` shows P8A "winning" (0.99+) on the same run that the lockbox-calibrated scorecard shows regressing −13.6 pp aggregate fake recall on `teams_fake_all_dev`. The contradiction is structural — the in-trainer composite uses dev-FPR-budget thresholds with a heuristic τ; the contract uses lockbox + lexicographic policy; same model, same data, two different τs, two different verdicts.
- **2026-04-26 [PACKET_9_MID_FLIGHT_HANDOFF](../../relaunch_handoffs/PACKET_9_MID_FLIGHT_HANDOFF_2026-04-26.md:50-52) names the rule explicitly:** *"Trainer's `value_composite` is **NOT** deployment-grade — multiple times we've crowned the wrong checkpoint by trusting it"*. This is the first place the "multiple crowning failures" pattern is acknowledged in a handoff (it had been implicit in the contract-policy bug whack-a-mole before). The handoff then prescribes the operational rule: "Crown via `arena/score_teams_promotion_contract.py:430-479` (lexicographic sort), **NOT** by trainer's `value_composite`."
- **2026-04-26 [PACKET_9_MID_FLIGHT_HANDOFF](../../relaunch_handoffs/PACKET_9_MID_FLIGHT_HANDOFF_2026-04-26.md:135-145) introduces `ood_composite` as a parallel directional metric.** Commit `3426bdf` ("Add P8A step 2500 (ood_composite) anchor rescore") — the team had been quoting both `value_composite` and `ood_composite` as if interchangeable; the handoff codifies them as **two separate directional readouts** with different aggregations. `ood_composite` weights OOD-stress pool AUCs; `value_composite` weights teams-fakes recall + stability. Neither is deployment-grade. Reading both in parallel is the trainer-side hygiene equivalent of "always check `selected_threshold` before trusting a contract scorecard."
- **2026-04-26 P9 crowning protocol pre-commits to lockbox readout** (`PACKET_9_MID_FLIGHT_HANDOFF_2026-04-26.md:134-147`). A P9 candidate is **promotable** only if all three hold on lockbox: `lockbox_real_fpr ≤ RLP6_04.lockbox_real_fpr` (≤ 0.441%); `lockbox_fake_recall ≥ max(0.80, RLP6_04 − 0.02)`; per-method recall on `deeplive_enhanced` ≥ 3.4% and `visomaster_enhanced_macro` ≥ 0.3% (RLP6_04 baseline minus 3pt slack). Hard cap 7% lockbox FPR. **Trainer composite is not in the rule set.** This is the first packet whose pre-launch contract names the readout-side gates explicitly and excludes `value_composite` by construction.
- **2026-04-26 phase-0-execution LOG entry: backbone_lr_mult plumbing** (`april-26-training-master-plan-v2.LOG.md:88-122` references commit `0f2f342`). The yaml lever `optimizer.adam.backbone_lr_mult` was added to soften P8A's unfreeze recipe. The discussion frame: "if value_composite stays at 0.99+ on a softened P8A but lockbox per-method recall recovers, that's the trade we want." The deployment-relevant target is the lockbox readout; `value_composite` is treated as a "must not collapse" floor (signaling the run is broken, not signaling the run is winning).
- **2026-04-26 LOG entry: phase-c-overnight-slate post-mortem** (`april-26-training-master-plan-v2.LOG.md:318-343`). On the A.2 v3 readout: *"contract policy bug fired on A.2 v3 as predicted (τ ≈ 0.99 across all 3 checkpoints; calibrated lockbox_fake_recall ~24%; defer to diagnostic τ=0.5 readout for promotion thinking)"*. The policy bug + composite-blindness pair is now a *pair* of failure modes — neither metric alone is deployment-grade; the diagnostic τ=0.5 readout is the operational fallback when the calibrator collapses. `value_composite` is not even in the diagnostic loop here; it's the metric that survived from training but is no longer consulted for promotion decisions.

## Current stance (2026-04-26)

`value_composite` is a **trainer-side directional metric** that does three things:

1. **Drives in-training checkpoint selection** — `best_value_composite.pth` is the canonical save target alongside `best_ood_composite.pth` (per RLP3 A2 dual-checkpoint, [RLP3.md](../packets/RLP3.md)). Without it, the trainer would have to pick by AUC alone, which has been shown to crown wrong checkpoints (e.g., RLP3 ranking flip, [`promotion_contract_evolution`](promotion_contract_evolution.md)).
2. **Provides a sanity floor** — a run whose `value_composite` collapses (e.g., to <0.5) is a broken run; the metric will surface that early and let the run be killed.
3. **Reports a directional comparison across packets** — comparing P3.5 (0.7442) to P3 (0.7232) to P5 (0.7736) to P6 (0.9006) to P8A (0.99+) shows training-side direction over time. Because the composite definition has shifted (RLP3.5 gate-definition update, RLP6 real-pool-only correction), absolute values across packets are not directly comparable; the relative direction within consecutive packets under the same gate is informative.

`value_composite` does **not** do these things:

1. **It does not detect the camera/ISP-signature shortcut** — RLP6_04 with `value_composite=0.9006` is concrete proof. The composite's real pools do not cover the deployment failure surface; same-person-different-camera flips do not show up in the metric.
2. **It does not survive a representation-collapse regression** — P8A with `value_composite=0.99+` is concrete proof. The composite reports "fake recall is high on the gate's training-time fake pools"; the lockbox per-method readout shows fake recall has collapsed on the methods that matter (`visomaster_enhanced_macro`, `deeplive_enhanced`) by −13.6 pp aggregate.
3. **It does not perform the lexicographic τ calibration** — the trainer's heuristic τ-binsearch (mean_fpr ≤ 0.03 + max_fpr ≤ 0.05) is fundamentally different from the contract's `_threshold_sort_key` (`arena/score_teams_promotion_contract.py:418-427`) which minimizes `dev_primary_real_fpr` first. The two τ values can diverge by 0.05+; the trainer τ stays in the 0.4–0.6 range under healthy gates, the contract τ has been observed at 0.995 (the τ-tail-collapse, [`promotion_contract_evolution`](promotion_contract_evolution.md)).
4. **It does not bind a deployment promotion** — operational rule lives in memory `project_promotion_contract.md`: dev-calibrated lexicographic τ + lockbox readout. `value_composite` is informational at promotion time.

The operational rule, codified in P9's crowning protocol: a checkpoint is **promotable** if the lockbox-anchored contract scorecard passes the per-method recall floors at a non-collapsed τ, *regardless* of the trainer composite. `value_composite` is the in-flight monitoring metric; the contract scorecard is the gate. Both `value_composite` and `ood_composite` should be quoted side-by-side at the in-flight reporting level — they aggregate different things and disagree informatively when something is off.

### Open loop: value-composite-cross-packet-comparability
status: open
severity: low
first_seen: 2026-04-22
last_verified: 2026-04-29
close_criterion: a per-packet table records which `value_composite` definition (gate thresholds, stability aggregator, real-pool set, fake-pool set) was active for the leader checkpoint, so that absolute composite numbers are quotable with provenance

`value_composite` has changed definition at least three times across the relaunch: (1) RLP3 origin `(mean_FPR ≤ 0.02, max_FPR ≤ 0.04, stability=max)`; (2) RLP3.5 gate-definition shift to `(0.03, 0.05, p95)`; (3) RLP6 real-pool-only `worst_pool_fpr` correction (avspeech + VCD removed from real-pool gate). Each change moved the absolute value of the composite for the same checkpoint substantially. The "0.7232 → 0.7442 → 0.7736 → 0.9006 → 0.99+" series across RLP3 → RLP3.5 → RLP5 → RLP6 → P8A is a **mix** of training-side direction and gate-definition mass; the threads address this case-by-case but the per-packet provenance is not surfaced as a single table. A future agent reading the handoffs sees the numbers without seeing which gate version computed them. Closing this loop would mean a small sidecar in each packet retro listing the active gate definition for the composite quoted; not load-bearing for the immediate Slice-5 narrative but useful for any future agent who wants to compare across the 6-week sequence.
