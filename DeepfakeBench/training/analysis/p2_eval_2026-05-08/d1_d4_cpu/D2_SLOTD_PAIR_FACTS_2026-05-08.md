## Direct observations

1. PC_Generator__s22 mean shift step6000 → step19000 = +0.5621 (largest absolute
   per-cohort delta in the 5-ckpt set on this substrate).
2. healthy_dor_shkedi mean shift = -0.3272 (only large negative delta among reals).
3. Roy_D, bla_bla_chow, PC_Generator__s45 — chronic identities — are at HIGH mean
   (>0.72) in BOTH `top_n_step6000` and `top_n_step19000`. The canary's `_step=18000`
   dip is not reproduced.
4. Deeplive fakes are at >0.95 mean in both ckpts (highest fake-mean cohort).
5. viso_fake mean shifted +0.2063 (step6000 0.484 → step19000 0.690).
6. The mapping between canary `_step` (multiples of 3000 in C/D logs) and saved-ckpt
   step labels (e.g. `top_n_step19000`) is unverified. Two interpretations are
   consistent with the data: (a) `top_n_step19000` corresponds to a state different
   from canary `_step=18000` by a few hundred optimizer-steps in either direction;
   (b) the canary's per-frame state at `_step=18000` was a transient that was not
   captured by any value-composite top_n ckpt selection.
