## Question

Does W&B `_step` correspond 1:1 to the optimizer-step labels in saved-ckpt filenames?
Which canary fires at `_step` ∈ {3000, 6000, 9000, 12000, 15000, 18000, 21000, 24000}
correspond to which saved Slot D ckpt under per-frame inference? Is the `_step=18000`
chronic-dip state captured by any saved ckpt?

## Method

Score 6 Slot D ckpts on the same 800-frame canary parquet (CPU PyTorch inference,
batch 32, ~47s per ckpt). For each ckpt compute the headline stats the in-training
canary logs (per `trainer/mixins/canary_probe.py`): score quantiles on reals/fakes,
lockbox_recall_at_FPR_{5,10}pct, max/mean per-identity mean score, chronic_mean/<id>
for the 6 chronic identities. Compare to logged canary fire stats via Euclidean
distance over 17 shared metrics; smallest distance = operational mapping.

## Direct observations

1. **`_step` == optimizer step at exact-match fires.** Canary `_step=3000` ↔ `step3000`
   L2=0.106; canary `_step=6000` ↔ `step6000` L2=0.131. Distances are within
   single-batch numerical noise across 17 metrics. The W&B `_step` axis and the
   optimizer-step labels in saved-ckpt filenames are the same scale.
2. **Slot D ran past the yaml's `total_training_steps: 8000` cap.** Saved top_n
   ckpts at step10500, 14500, 19000 exist; the canary fired through `_step=24000`.
   The yaml field did not enforce a hard stop at this run.
3. **The `_step=18000` chronic-dip state is closest to `slotD_periodic_step3000`
   among saved ckpts (L2=0.437).** That distance is ~4× larger than the exact-match
   distances (0.10-0.13), so the dip state is NOT directly reproducible from any
   saved ckpt; the closest savable approximation is the early step3000 ckpt.
4. **`lockbox_recall_at_FPR_10pct` is highest at `step3000`**: 0.37 vs 0.06-0.20 at
   later ckpts. Slot D's 6 saved ckpt values:
   - step3000: 0.37
   - step6000: 0.06
   - step8000: 0.12
   - step10500: 0.07
   - step14500: 0.10
   - step19000: 0.20
   Trajectory is non-monotone in optimizer step.
5. **`score_p95_on_reals` saturates >0.99 at every ckpt except step3000.** step3000
   score_p95=0.883; all later ckpts 0.990-0.994. step3000 is the only saved ckpt
   with sub-0.95 real-side p95.
6. **Chronic-6 mean trajectory across the 6 saved ckpts**:
   - PC_Generator__s22: 0.282 → 0.223 → 0.259 → 0.282 → 0.704 → 0.785
   - PC_Generator__s45: 0.587 → 0.678 → 0.702 → 0.677 → 0.720 → 0.728
   - Q__s6: 0.394 → 0.342 → 0.334 → 0.317 → 0.633 → 0.286
   - Roy_D: 0.757 → 0.931 → 0.956 → 0.969 → 0.975 → 0.940
   - bla_bla_chow: 0.359 → 0.780 → 0.813 → 0.796 → 0.854 → 0.882
   - bla_bla_chow__s2: 0.375 → 0.292 → 0.462 → 0.503 → 0.769 → 0.516
   Step3000 is the cohort minimum on Roy_D, bla_bla_chow, and PC_Generator__s22.
7. **`step14500` produces an off-trajectory state**: reals_p50=0.445 vs the others'
   0.140-0.227, and chronic_mean across all 6 identities all > 0.6 (vs the others
   showing diversity). step14500 closest match in the canary fires is `_step=15000`
   (L2=0.345).
8. **`step8000` (yaml's nominal cap) is the closest saved match to canary `_step=9000`**
   (L2=0.210). step8000's chronic-real means resemble step6000 + a small drift toward
   higher saturation.

## Implication for Phase A scorecard ckpt selection

The mapping clarifies which saved ckpts are candidates for capturing distinct training
states:

- **`step3000`** — distinct distribution (sub-0.95 score_p95_on_reals); highest
  `lockbox_recall@FPR_10pct` (0.37); chronic minima on Roy_D, bla_bla_chow,
  PC_Generator__s22; closest savable approximation to canary `_step=18000` dip.
- **`step19000`** — closest match to the late-training-saturated state (canary
  `_step=21000-24000`); highest training-AUC (0.9908); high chronic activations.
- **`step6000`, `step8000`, `step10500`** — middle-trajectory; small per-metric
  differences from each other (L2 0.24-0.30 between adjacent pairs).
- **`step14500`** — off-trajectory: simultaneously high real-side p50 (0.445) AND
  high fake-side activations; matches canary `_step=15000` only.

The canary `_step=18000` chronic-dip state CANNOT be directly Phase-A-scored from a
saved ckpt; the closest available savable approximation is `step3000`.
