# Thread: Processing-signature shortcut

## The question

Has the detector learned to classify the *capture/encoding/pipeline signature* attached to a video rather than the *face-artifact content* of the frame? If yes, deployment is structurally blocked: two sessions of the same person on two different webcams can sit on opposite sides of the decision boundary without any identity or manipulation change.

## Initial belief

Through [RLP3](../packets/RLP3.md) the going assumption was that residual OOD failures were lighting- or stress-driven. The packet's A3/A3b lanes (deterministic lighting + spatial stress) framed the problem as an invariance / robustness gap. Slot-07 in RLP3 (a 0.40-scale lighting probe) was where the lighting-aug reading started to look like a symptom of something larger — but the framing stayed "augmentation and training distribution are the levers" through [RLP3.5](../packets/RLP3_5.md) and [RLP4](../packets/RLP4.md).

## What changed our mind

- [RLP5](../packets/RLP5.md) slot-07 A/B on the Dor annotated manifest. On `dor_shkedi` (1138 lockbox fakes) the checkpoint outputs `mean prob_fake = 0.457` with 14.6% > 0.9; on `real_dor` (109 frames of the *same face*, different `identity_key`) it outputs `mean = 0.038` with 0% > 0.9. Same person, different pipeline, flipped output. Codified in memory `project_signature_shortcut_finding.md`.
- [RLP6](../packets/RLP6.md) slot-04 post-deployment 2-camera controlled test (`R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET7_CAMERA_SIGNATURE_HANDOFF_2026-04-24.md:5-19`): Dor laptop no-VB → 0.02; Dor webcam same scene no-VB → 0.94; Roee Mac webcam with VB → 0.90; Roee Windows laptop → 0.01. Identity held fixed, camera/OS swap flipped the score. Undeniable.
- [WS-P0](../packets/WS_probes.md) post-fix re-score (`analysis/rlp6_04_postfix_rescore_2026-04-24.summary.json`). Anchor pool `dor-real-webcam-false-flag-no-virtual-bg` moved only −0.008 (0.940 → 0.932) after the `INTER_LINEAR` fix. Preprocessing drift ruled out as the cause; `decision_tree.verdict = "fully_structural__launch_full_subset"`. The shortcut is a *learned representation*, not an inference-path artifact.
- Fingerprint diagnostics (`analysis/dor_baseline_repro_2026-04-24.summary.json`, `dor_pool_fingerprints_2026-04-24.summary.json`) ranked `dct_hf_ratio` (max_sep 3.46), `wb_rb_ratio` (2.32), `mean_cb` (2.23), `mean_cr` (1.97) as the most consistent pool-separating signals — two codec-aligned, two chroma/lighting. Camera/ISP signature was made numerically legible.

Memory `project_p8a_frame_level_auc_2026-04-29.md` codifies the operational rule: *"Always sanity-check operating-point recall against frame-level AUC before concluding a checkpoint is bad. ## Current stance (2026-04-29)

The detector has learned a **camera/ISP processing signature** that is layered on top of a working cross-domain fake-detection capability — not a manipulation-content boundary, but also not a model-of-nothing as the contract scorecard's near-zero recall numbers had implied. The Slice-7 reframing collapses three previously-distinct findings into one consistent picture: (a) the model's frame-level cross-domain AUC is meaningfully high (P8A: viso 0.75 / deeplive 0.86 / teams_fake 0.91); (b) the contract scorer's pre-fix τ-policy was reading near-zero recall as a **scorer artifact**, not a model failure; (c) the camera-signature shortcut is real (Axis 3 Δ ~0.5 on best P13) and is the **residual deployment block** even after the contract-policy fix and bucket-gap closure both land. Direct consequences:

- Lockbox 90/90 on RLP6_04 is **not** threshold-reachable: at τ catching 90% of fakes, real FPR is 63.6%. This is a representation ceiling, not a calibration gap. Trainer's `value_composite` cannot see it (see [promotion_contract_evolution](promotion_contract_evolution.md), [`value_composite_semantics`](value_composite_semantics.md)).
- Training-aug is the dominant remaining lever ([calibration_vs_training_aug](calibration_vs_training_aug.md) — WS-P1 closed only 30.1% of the gap).
- **P8A's CLIP backbone unfreeze (proj + ln_post + MLP-SVD) breaks the P7 anchor ceiling** (Δ=−0.188 vs RLP6_04, ~2× the best P7 result), but at the cost of −13.6 pp aggregate fake recall on `teams_fake_all_dev`. Failure-mode analysis says the regression is separability loss, not threshold drift — lockbox τ-tuning will not rescue it. Implication: the soften axes (P9_01 magnitude, P9_03 topology, P9_05 data) are the right next moves; the trade-off is real and may force a different intervention class entirely (P10 anti-shortcut packet — symmetric router + GRL slate — is drafted as the orthogonal alternative).
- **P8B refuted "the shortcut is in the weight chain"** — scratch on plain CLIP performed *worse* than RLP6_04. Combined with P8A: the shortcut is **data-bound**; capacity helps; scratch is wrong. Data-side intervention is the only path to *eliminate* the shortcut (e.g., camera-diversified Teams real captures), but P8A's recipe goes a long way without one.
- Per-identity reducer (WS-P2.b) is the diagnosis primitive: any RLP7+ checkpoint can be sliced to surface "did this variant manufacture a new Dor?" without ad-hoc analysis.
- The "FT-only is structurally bounded" P7 ceiling was measured **with broken in_proj-SVD** (every R12g / RLP / P-* run with `apply_svd_to_in_proj: true` trained the q/k/v residuals on zero classification gradient until commit `2feea58` 2026-04-26). Post-fix, the FT lever set is meaningfully larger; the ceiling has not been re-measured from a clean fork point. See [`in_proj_svd_gradient_bug`](in_proj_svd_gradient_bug.md).
- **The shortcut is now understood as multi-axis, not single-axis.** The Slice-6 evidence converges on three interlocking shortcut axes: (1) camera/ISP signature (Slice-4 origin), (2) capture-mode tail (lockbox webcam mode = 65.7% FPR; see [`webcam_fpr_dominance`](webcam_fpr_dominance.md)), (3) face-pixel-area label leak (per-method face-size single-point distributions; see [`face_size_label_leak`](face_size_label_leak.md)). The crop-tightness sweep (53% prediction-flip rate across t∈[0.7, 1.5]) demonstrates that all three are entangled at the per-frame level — perturbing geometric crop alone flips real ↔ fake on the same person. Training-aug interventions must address all three axes; P11 attacked face-size + codec; P13 stacks anchor-aware loss + pipeline-random aug + face-scale jitter (build-only this slice; verdict Slice 7).
- **Inference-time stack is exhausted as a recipe lever** (Option A subagent verdict 2026-04-28 13:30). 85 calibration + ensemble configurations on existing P8A + 4 P11 predictions all fail the 90/5 gate; AUC(visomaster fake vs modern_v2 real) caps at ~0.65. The substrate is bounded; redesign-class intervention is required (P13 anchor-aware) or scratch-retrain.
