## Where the Anti-Shortcut Interventions Did and Didn't Move the Needle

**Did move (positive signal):**
- Axis 3 shortcut Δ: P13_step18000 = **0.520** vs P8A = 0.659 vs RLP6_04 = 0.830. ~14pp improvement over P8A on the same-face-different-pipeline probe. Step18000 is consistently the best P13 by Axis 3 (the late-training ckpts do show convergence on this metric).
- In-domain lockbox fake recall: P13_step18000 = 88.5% at τ=0.5 (best of all candidates).

**Did NOT move (clear negatives):**
- Cross-domain fake detection (viso/deeplive/teams_fake_dev): P13 is **worst** at every τ-policy. From-scratch lost the cross-domain priors that P8A inherited.
- Modern_v2 real FPR: P13 at τ=0.5 sits 23–40% — a webcam-real frame catches "fake" with high confidence on a third of inputs. Worse than P8A (3.6%) and RLP6_04 (20%).
- The shortcut Δ is still 3.5× the 0.15 gate — anchor-aware loss + pipeline-randomization + face scale-jitter were not strong enough on their own to break it.

The from-scratch experiment isolates the shortcut intervention, but at the cost of cross-domain capability. The next iteration needs the **same interventions on top of a P8A-quality base.**
