## Question

For each pair of {Slot A top_n_step500, Slot B top_n_step500, Slot C top_n_step7000,
Slot D top_n_step6000, Slot D top_n_step19000, P8A_step5000}, what is the per-tensor
L2 delta and cosine similarity, grouped by transformer block?

## Method

For each common tensor `t` between two ckpts A and B (matched on key, shape, and dtype):

- `l2_delta(t) = ||t_A - t_B||_2`
- `rel_l2_delta(t) = l2_delta(t) / max(||t_A||_2, 1e-12)`
- `cosine(t) = cos(t_A.flatten(), t_B.flatten())`

Aggregated by transformer block (`embed`, `layernorm_outer`, `resblock_00`..`resblock_11`,
`visual_proj`, `head`, `other`).

15 pairs × 16 blocks = 240 summary rows. 598 tensors per ckpt. P8A reference at
`gs://training-job-outputs/phase2r13_experiments/9lmvb5b4/value_composite_effort_20260424_step5000_auc0.9926_eer0.0270.pth`.

## Direct observations (numeric only)

1. `slotA_top_n_step500` and `slotB_top_n_step500` are nearly identical across every
   block: `min_cosine ≥ 0.98` everywhere, `rel_l2 ≤ 0.056` (peak at visual_proj), and
   `≤ 0.018` on resblock_11. Both early-stopped at optimizer step 500 with similar
   final training-AUC values (0.6802 vs 0.6800).
2. `visual_proj` block: P2 vs P8A `rel_l2 ≈ 1.0-1.6` with `min_cosine ≤ 0.05` for the
   from-scratch pairs (A/B/C/D vs P8A `min_cosine` 0.03-0.19). A/B vs C/D have
   `rel_l2 ≈ 1.001` and `min_cosine ≤ 0.007` (near-orthogonal directions among the
   from-scratch P2 ckpts).
3. `visual_proj`: among the 4 trained P2 ckpts, the C/D-pair set is the only one with
   `min_cosine > 0.89` to each other. C-vs-Dstep6000 = 0.917, C-vs-Dstep19000 = 0.899,
   Dstep6000-vs-Dstep19000 = 0.946. A/B-vs-{C,D} are all near 0.005.
4. `resblock_11`: D step19000 has `min_cosine` in the negative range (-0.273 to -0.280)
   against A, B, and P8A. D step6000 and C step7000 vs D step19000 have `min_cosine`
   ~0.025-0.034 (orthogonal). The same 49 tensors of D's last layer pre-training
   (D step6000) and post-training (D step19000) have moved through ~90° of cosine angle.
5. `embed` block has `rel_l2 = 0` across all 15 pairs, confirming the patch embedding
   layer was not modified by any P2 or P8A training. SVD-residual training operates only
   on `transformer.resblocks.*.{attn,mlp}` + `visual.proj` + `visual.ln_post` + `head`.
