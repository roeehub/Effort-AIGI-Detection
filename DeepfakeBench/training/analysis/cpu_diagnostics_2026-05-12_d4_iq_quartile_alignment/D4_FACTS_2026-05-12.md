> **Question tested**: per-frame `prob_fake` per ckpt is thresholded at the dev-calibrated 5% FPR τ. For each of 6 IQ axes, dev-derived quartile cut points are applied to both `teams_real_all_dev` and `teams_real_all_lockbox` real frames. Per-quartile FPR is measured on each substrate and compared via Pearson r and mean-abs-delta across the 4 quartile cells.

## 1. Method

**Input matrix**: `analysis/cpu_diagnostics_2026-05-12_stage_a/outputs/unified_frame_matrix.csv` (13,636 rows; columns include `suite`, `label`, scores for `P8A`, `T5C_step3500`, `T3_S1_step1500`, and 6 IQ axes).

**Substrates**
- `teams_real_all_dev`: n = 4,564 rows, all `label == 0`.
- `teams_real_all_lockbox`: n = 1,418 rows, all `label == 0`.

**IQ atlas coverage on these substrates** (all 6 axes non-null)
- dev: 1,623 / 4,564 = 35.6%.
- lockbox: 597 / 1,418 = 42.1%.

**6 IQ axes used**: `min_dim`, `lap_var`, `luma_mean`, `saturation_mean`, `color_a_dev`, `color_b_dev`.

**3 ckpts scored**: `P8A`, `T5C_step3500`, `T3_S1_step1500`.

**τ calibration (per ckpt)**: τ is set to the 95th-percentile (1 − 0.05 quantile) of the score distribution on the full `teams_real_all_dev` real cohort. Sanity check FPR on the same dev cohort at this τ: 0.0502 for all three ckpts (single shared cohort; quantile method).

| Ckpt | τ@5%FPR (dev) | FPR on full dev real (sanity) | FPR on full lockbox real |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P8A | 0.9752 | 0.0502 | **0.0092** |
| T5C_step3500 | 0.8465 | 0.0502 | **0.0240** |
| T3_S1_step1500 | 0.7729 | 0.0502 | **0.0219** |

**Per-axis procedure**: drop rows with null IQ on the axis; compute dev-derived 25/50/75 percentile cut points; place each dev and each lockbox real frame into the dev-defined quartile bin; compute fraction-of-scores `> τ` per bin (FPR); record dev_fpr, lockbox_fpr, and (dev_fpr − lockbox_fpr) per cell.

**Alignment metric (per ckpt × axis)**: Pearson r between the 4-vector (dev Q1..Q4 FPR) and (lockbox Q1..Q4 FPR); plus mean(|dev_qX_fpr − lockbox_qX_fpr|) across the 4 cells.

**Joint-bin sanity**: 4 cells from median split on (`lap_var`, `min_dim`); dev medians define cuts; lockbox placed into same cells.

**Lockbox-like-dev**: per axis, find the lockbox 25th percentile (lockbox-Q1 upper bound); compute FPR on dev frames at-or-below this cut, compare to lockbox-Q1 FPR.

**Scripts**: `run_d4.py` (single file, CPU only, no n_jobs=-1).

## 7. Caveats

- IQ atlas join coverage on the input matrix is ~50.6% project-wide; on the dev/lockbox real subsets used here it is 35.6% (dev) / 42.1% (lockbox). Per-axis quartile cells use rows with non-null IQ on the axis, so each axis-conditioned analysis is on n ≈ 1,623 dev / 597 lockbox.
- Per-quartile lockbox cell sizes are uneven because the dev-derived cut points are applied to lockbox. Lockbox concentrates in mid-`min_dim` (Q3 has 369/597 = 61.8%) and high-`lap_var` (Q3 has 412/597 = 69.0%); some lockbox quartile cells thus have n as low as 14 (color_b_dev Q2) or 26 (luma_mean Q4). Low-n cells contribute noise to per-quartile FPR estimates; this affects mean |Δ| and Pearson r at the per-axis level.
- τ is calibrated on `teams_real_all_dev` only; this is consistent with the "dev-calibrated 5% FPR" lever used in prior packets (e.g., the deployment τ-relax studies). The lockbox FPRs at this τ are 0.0092 (P8A) / 0.0240 (T5C_step3500) / 0.0219 (T3_S1_step1500) — all below 5% — so the overall direction is **lockbox is easier than dev** at the dev-calibrated τ for all three ckpts on this matrix.
- Pearson r on 4 cells is high-variance; a single-cell flip can swing r by >0.5. The aggregate per-ckpt r (mean across 6 axes) is more stable but still rests on a sample of 6 × 4 = 24 cells per ckpt.
- IQ axes are correlated (e.g., per D1, joint R² < sum of univariate r² on chronic-6 reals); per-axis quartile binning therefore does not isolate independent IQ subspaces. The joint (lap_var × min_dim) median split in Section 4 is a 2-axis sanity check, not a full multivariate matching procedure.
- The unified frame matrix was produced upstream; this script does no re-scoring. Any score-side issues (e.g., the IQ atlas join 50.6% retention) are inherited.
- `teams_real_all_lockbox` is n = 1,418 total reals; only 597 have full IQ. The 597-with-IQ subset may differ systematically from the 821-without-IQ subset in ways this analysis cannot probe.

## 8. Direct observations

1. At dev-calibrated 5% FPR τ, the headline FPR on `teams_real_all_lockbox` is **below 5%** for all three ckpts: P8A 0.92%, T5C_step3500 2.40%, T3_S1_step1500 2.19%. The dev-to-lockbox direction is dev → easier on lockbox in aggregate.
2. Per-ckpt aggregate Pearson r (mean across 6 axes) between dev-quartile-FPR and lockbox-quartile-FPR vectors: **P8A +0.716, T5C_step3500 +0.508, T3_S1_step1500 −0.032**. Per-ckpt mean |Δ|: **P8A 0.026, T5C_step3500 0.050, T3_S1_step1500 0.046**.
3. For P8A, 5 of 6 axes have per-axis Pearson r ≥ +0.35; the lowest is `lap_var` at r = +0.129. For T5C_step3500, 1 axis is negative (`luma_mean` r = −0.517). For T3_S1_step1500, 4 of 6 axes have negative per-axis r.
4. The single largest absolute dev↔lockbox FPR delta per ckpt sits on a high-IQ-deviation Q4 cell for two of three ckpts: T3_S1_step1500 `color_a_dev` Q4 |Δ| = 0.143 (dev 0.143 / lockbox 0.000), T5C_step3500 `color_a_dev` Q4 |Δ| = 0.142 (dev 0.185 / lockbox 0.042). For P8A the largest |Δ| is on `saturation_mean` Q1 at 0.066 (dev 0.059 / lockbox 0.125) — the only direction-reversed top cell, with lockbox FPR higher than dev FPR.
5. In the joint (`lap_var` × `min_dim`) median-split sanity check, the `lap_hi · md_hi` bin (most lockbox mass: 409 / 597 = 68.5%) has dev_fpr 0.0066 / 0.0464 / 0.0232 and lockbox_fpr 0.0024 / 0.0049 / 0.0073 for P8A / T5C / T3_S1 respectively. The `lap_lo · md_lo` bin has dev_fpr 0.0264 / 0.0891 / 0.0528 and lockbox_fpr 0.0652 / 0.0217 / 0.0435 — for P8A the joint-bin direction reverses here (lockbox higher than dev), consistent with the P8A `saturation_mean` Q1 observation above.
6. Lockbox-like-dev test (dev frames whose axis value is at or below lockbox-Q1 25th-percentile cut, FPR compared to lockbox-Q1 FPR at the same τ):
   - P8A: 3 / 6 axes meet |Δ| ≤ 0.02 (`lap_var`, `saturation_mean`, `color_b_dev`).
   - T5C_step3500: 2 / 6 axes meet the threshold (`saturation_mean`, `color_a_dev`).
   - T3_S1_step1500: 4 / 6 axes meet the threshold (`min_dim`, `saturation_mean`, `color_a_dev`, `color_b_dev`).
7. The lockbox-like-dev `|Δ|` exceeds 0.05 in 4 of 18 (ckpt × axis) cells, all on `luma_mean`, `min_dim`, or `color_b_dev`. The maximum is T5C_step3500 `luma_mean` (dev 0.1010 / lockbox 0.0067 = +0.0944).
8. Aggregate magnitudes per ckpt: P8A has the strictest dev↔lockbox alignment by both metrics (highest r = +0.716, lowest mean |Δ| = 0.026); T3_S1_step1500 has the loosest (r = −0.032, mean |Δ| = 0.046).
