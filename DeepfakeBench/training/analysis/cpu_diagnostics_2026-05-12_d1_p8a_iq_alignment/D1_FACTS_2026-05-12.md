## 1. Method

**Input data**

- Per-frame matrix: `analysis/cpu_diagnostics_2026-05-12_stage_a/outputs/unified_frame_matrix.csv` (13,636 rows; columns `P8A`, `label`, `is_chronic_6`, `frame_path`, 6 IQ axes + others)
- IQ atlas join coverage in the matrix: 6,905 / 13,636 = 50.6% rows have all 6 IQ axes non-null.

**6 IQ axes used**: `min_dim`, `lap_var`, `luma_mean`, `saturation_mean`, `color_a_dev`, `color_b_dev`.

**Score transform**: `P8A_logit = log(p / (1 − p))` with `p` clipped to `[1e-6, 1 − 1e-6]`.

**Feature standardization**: each axis z-scored (mean 0, std 1, ddof=0) prior to OLS so β-coefficients are directly comparable in magnitude.

**OLS implementation**: closed-form via numpy (intercept + 6 axes, pseudoinverse normal equations). p-values + 95% CIs computed via two-sided t with df = n − 7 (`scipy.stats.t`). R² reported via `sklearn.linear_model.LinearRegression` for cross-check; matches OLS by construction.

**Identity extraction (A3)**: `frame_path.split('/')[-1].split('__')[0]` (basename pre-first-`__`).

**Filters**
- A1: `label == 0 AND is_chronic_6 == 1 AND all 6 IQ axes non-null` → n = 2,436.
- A4: `label == 0 AND is_chronic_6 == 0 AND all 6 IQ axes non-null` → n = 1,448.
- A3: A1 cohort, then per-identity-pattern `contains` match on extracted identity; identities with n < 30 reported as skipped.

**Scripts**: `run_d1.py` (single-file).

## 7. Caveats

- IQ atlas join coverage is 50.6% on the unified frame matrix; rows with any null IQ axis are dropped before regression. A1 cohort therefore conditions on `iq_join == ok` (n = 2,436 of 5,373 total chronic-6 real rows; 45.3% of chronic-6 reals retained).
- A4 cohort (healthy reals with non-null IQ) is **n = 1,448**. A1 (chronic-6 reals, same filter) is **n = 2,436**. Cohort sizes differ ~1.7×.
- The `healthy_dor` pattern returned zero matches in the chronic-6-flagged real cohort. The identities actually present in `is_chronic_6 == 1 AND label == 0`, by basename-prefix counts (full chronic-6 cohort before IQ filter), are: `PC_Generator` (1,729), `dor_shkedi` (1,263), `bla_bla_chow` (1,071), `Md_noyn_Sharker` (1,014), `Roy_D` (246), plus a long tail of frame-named rows whose basename did not split on `__` cleanly (e.g., `dor_shkedi_real_frame_000311_seq2841.jpg`). The `dor_shkedi` row in A3 captures the clean-split tier; `healthy_dor` is reported as n=0 because no matching basename was found.
- R² is in-sample (no held-out split). 95% CIs assume IID residuals; per-identity correlation in residuals is plausible (chronic-6 cohort is dominated by ~5 identities) so reported CIs may under-cover the true population uncertainty.
- Sum of univariate r² > joint R² (0.4247 > 0.3499) indicates the 6 IQ axes carry overlapping (correlated) information; joint R² is the appropriate measure of total IQ-explained variance.

## 8. Direct observations

1. Joint R² of P8A logit on the 6 IQ axes is **0.3499** on chronic-6 reals (n = 2,436); the dominant standardized coefficient is on `saturation_mean` at β = +4.27 with 95% CI [+3.86, +4.67].
2. All six axes have 95% CIs that exclude zero on the A1 cohort; the two largest-magnitude joint-β axes are `saturation_mean` (+4.27) and `color_b_dev` (−3.53), each ≈ 2-4× larger in magnitude than the next-tier axes (`color_a_dev`, `min_dim`, `luma_mean`).
3. The dominant axis under joint regression (`saturation_mean`, |β| = 4.27) is the second-weakest axis under univariate regression (Pearson r = −0.14, r² = 0.0186); this is consistent with multicollinearity among IQ axes.
4. Per-identity R² range across the 5 fitted chronic-6 sub-cohorts is **0.1748 to 0.7574**; highest on PC_Generator (R² = 0.7574, n = 863), lowest on Md_noyn_Sharker (R² = 0.1748, n = 372).
5. The dominant-axis sign reverses across identities: `saturation_mean` β is negative on Roy_D (−6.15) but positive on dor_shkedi (+3.33); `color_b_dev` is negative on PC_Generator (−1.74) but positive on bla_bla_chow (+5.12).
6. Joint R² on the healthy-real baseline (A4) is **0.1629** (n = 1,448); on the chronic-6 cohort (A1) it is **0.3499** (n = 2,436). The chronic-6 R² is **higher than** the healthy baseline R² by 0.187 absolute (≈ 2.15× ratio).
7. The dominant joint-β axis is `saturation_mean` in both cohorts, but with opposite signs: +4.27 on chronic-6 vs −1.08 on healthy; |β| is ≈ 3.9× larger on chronic-6.
