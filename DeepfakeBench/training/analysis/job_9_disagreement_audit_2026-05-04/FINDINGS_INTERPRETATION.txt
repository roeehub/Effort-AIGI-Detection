    AGREE_HIGH and 33.4 in AGREE_LOW). 76% of those 85 are raw subtype.
    On reals, P8A unique-FPs are 91% low-resolution and 86% chronic
    PC_Generator__s22/s45/Q/Test_Cam.
    E2B's 1 unique catch is teams subtype.

  Two competing reads (do not pick without the user):

    Opinion read A (P8A-favourable):
      "P8A is sensitive to a sharpness-correlated cue that fires on the
      raw subtype's video stream and on chronic-offender real frames.
      The cue is real on viso fakes and spurious on those reals --
      classical sharpness/edge shortcut documented in the image-quality
      shortcut memory entry. P8A 'works' on viso raw because its
      shortcut happens to align with raw-subtype's image statistics."

    Opinion read B (E2B-favourable):
      "E2B's lower viso scores reflect that it is NOT relying on the

  whether you believe a high-FP-on-low-res-PC_Generator-frames answer

    On dev reals: P8A FPs concentrate on PC_Generator__s22/s45 (83%
    chronic). E3 FPs concentrate on bla_bla_chow / Roy_D (93% chronic).
    Different chronic clusters.

  Opinion read:
    "P8A and E3 each have their own chronic-offender failure mode
    (PC_Generator family for P8A; bla_bla_chow + Roy_D for E3). They
    are picking up different shortcut axes, not the same one at
    different thresholds. An ensemble (e.g. min-rule) would only help
    if the two axes are statistically uncorrelated on real-side
    populations -- worth checking, given that current data shows
    near-zero overlap of FP identities between the two."


E2B vs E3 (one-sided viso disagreement)

  Numbers:
    DIS_A_HIGH_B_LOW = 0,  DIS_A_LOW_B_HIGH = 35.
    E2B never scores a viso fake high while E3 scores it low; the

  Opinion read:
    "E2B's score distribution is consistently more conservative than
    E3's. E3 is detecting a sharpness-related axis on raw viso (that

    cleaner real side AND a much smaller fake-side recall. Whether
    the trade is worth it depends on what specific viso pattern E2B
    is supposed to be detecting -- likely the teams-subtype, given
    that E2B is the only ckpt that scored a teams-subtype viso fake
    high while another ckpt scored it low."


Cross-pair pattern that ties them together
------------------------------------------
The viso DISAGREE_HIGH_LOW bands (P8A's 85, P8A's 37, E3's 35) are all
characterised by ELEVATED laplacian_var (sharpness) relative to
AGREE_LOW and to AGREE_HIGH within each pair. The same axis (sharpness)
correlates with the chronic-offender FP clusters on dev reals:
PC_Generator__s22/s45 and Q__s6 are higher-sharpness webcam captures;
bla_bla_chow / Roy_D are phone_screen / screen captures with sharper
edges from screen pixel grids.

That is consistent with the existing image-quality shortcut memory
entry: each of the three ckpts has its own sharpness-shortcut "tail",
just on slightly different points in the input distribution.

What is genuinely different per ckpt:
  * P8A's tail aligns most strongly with raw viso fakes AND with
    PC_Generator/Q/Test_Cam dev reals (mostly webcam, mostly low-res).
  * E3's tail aligns with a DIFFERENT raw-viso sub-population AND with
    bla_bla_chow/Roy_D (phone_screen/screen, larger).
  * E2B has a much smaller tail in either viso direction; its real-side
    FPs straddle both clusters at low absolute count.

This view is INTERPRETATION, not fact. The fact-side is in
FINDINGS_FACTS.txt.


What this analysis CANNOT decide
--------------------------------
1. Whether P8A's shortcut hurts deployment more than E3's, or vice versa.
   Both shortcuts manifest as chronic-offender FP clusters; deciding
   between them needs out-of-sample validation on the production fleet,
   not more analysis of the same dev/lockbox split.

2. Whether the teams-subtype gap (E2B being the only ckpt with any
   unique teams-subtype catch, n=1) is a meaningful signal or noise.
   n=1 is too small to call.

3. Which pair is the "best" ensemble candidate. Job 9 reports the
   disagreement skeleton; ensemble math (e.g. union-of-min-rule recall
