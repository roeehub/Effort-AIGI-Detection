## Configuration

P13 is the **substrate-redesign track** of Plan v4 (Track G), authored after the 2026-04-28 13:30 Option A subagent verdict γ confirmed that inference-time stacks could not break the substrate ceiling (85 calibration + ensemble configurations, all 85 fail the 90/5 gate; `april-26-training-master-plan-v2.LOG.md:1337-1383`). The hypothesis: P11/P12-class recipe-tuning had exhausted training-time aug levers on a P8A-rooted FT chain; the question is whether **starting from scratch** on the P8A architecture topology + applying three structurally-different anti-shortcut levers can break the camera-signature shortcut that input-space-only interventions could not.

### Variants (1 production + checkpointed across 8 steps)

- **R13_P13_FROM_SCRATCH.yaml** (commit `cab2909`, 2026-04-28). Same P8A architecture (CLIP-DataComp-XL backbone, `unfreeze_final_proj=true`, `unfreeze_final_ln=true`, `apply_svd_to_mlp=true`, `apply_svd_to_in_proj=true` post-fix `2feea58`), but training from scratch (no P8A init) with three new top-level yaml blocks:

  ```yaml
  anchor_aware:
    enabled: true
    weight: 5.0
    target_mean_prob: 0.10
    samples_per_step: 16

  pipeline_random:
    enabled: true
    apply_to_real: true
    apply_to_fake: true
    transforms: [jpeg, downscale, chroma_blur, yuv_roundtrip, gamma]

  face_scale_jitter:
    enabled: true
    scale_limit: 0.25
  ```

  These are the three levers that input-space recipe-tuning (P11 face-size + codec axis; P10 symmetric router) had been unable to combine because each was implemented as a separate ad-hoc patch.

### The wandb-flattening artifact and `c366026` precondition

The first P13 launch (2026-04-28 ~17:50 CEST, jobs `99039952980934656` + `8345130870696312832` us-east1 image 1.3.224 then 1.3.225) had **all three new yaml blocks silently DISABLED** by the wandb-flattening bug (memory `project_wandb_flattens_nested_dicts.md`). Trainer init logs printed `AnchorAwarePenalty DISABLED (enabled=False weight=5.0)` + `FaceScaleJitter DISABLED` despite the yaml asserting `enabled: true`. ~$3.50 was burned across 2 cancelled jobs; the first wrong-layered fix attempt (trainer-side `_to_plain_dict()` helper) lost ~$1.40.

The `c366026` commit ("Fix wandb-flattening: re-apply anchor_aware/face_scale_jitter/periodic_saves") added the three new keys to `train_sweep.py:283-313` allowlist; image 1.3.226 (commit `6c9a320`). The post-fix relaunch is what produced the verdict that lands here. **This is the first packet where all three Plan-v4 substrate-redesign levers actually fired.** See [`wandb_flattening`](../threads/wandb_flattening.md).

### Where the anti-shortcut interventions did and didn't move the needle

**Did move (positive signal):**
- Axis 3 shortcut Δ: P13_step18000 = **0.520** vs P8A = 0.659 vs RLP6_04 = 0.830. ~14pp improvement over P8A on the same-face-different-pipeline probe.
- In-domain lockbox fake recall: P13_step18000 = 88.5% at τ=0.5 (best of all candidates).

**Did NOT move (clear negatives):**
- Cross-domain fake detection (viso/deeplive/teams_fake_dev): P13 is **worst** at every τ-policy. From-scratch lost the cross-domain priors that P8A inherited.
- Modern_v2 real FPR: P13 at τ=0.5 sits 23-40%. Worse than P8A (3.6%) and RLP6_04 (20%).
- The shortcut Δ is still 3.5× the 0.15 gate. Anchor-aware loss + pipeline-rand + face scale-jitter were not strong enough on their own to break it.

## Retrospective (as of 2026-04-29)

P13 is the **first packet where all three Plan-v4 substrate-redesign levers actually fired**, and the **first packet whose verdict directly triggered a higher-level retrospective audit** (the bucket-gap + identity-diversity audits). The γ verdict is honest: from-scratch broke the cross-domain capability that P8A had, while moving the shortcut directionally but not far enough. The Day-5 fallback (P14 FT-from-P8A + interventions) is currently in flight; the Slice-7 bucket-gap finding revises the priority ordering — `R13_P14_DATA_FIX.yaml` (bucket-fix retrain) is the cheaper-first lift, drafted but not launched.

**Three structural bugs interact with P13 attribution:**

1. **The wandb-flattening bug** — silent-DISABLED all three new yaml blocks on the first launch (~$3.50 burned + first wrong-layered fix attempt at the trainer layer). Closed by `c366026` allowlist re-apply (3 keys) + `tests/test_train_sweep_reapply_allowlist.py` regression test. See [`wandb_flattening`](../threads/wandb_flattening.md).
2. **The contract policy bug** — buggy policy crowned `P13_step2000` rank 1 with 3.6% lockbox fake recall. The verdict had to manually re-run with non-degenerate τ policies to confirm γ. The v3 fix (`target_fake_recall_min=0.30`) is in working tree, uncommitted as of 2026-04-29. See [`contract_policy_bug`](../threads/contract_policy_bug.md).
3. **The bucket gap** — `visomaster_enhanced_macro_dev` reads from a Teams-recapture bucket that has no presence in any current training yaml. Cross-domain viso recall has been bounded above by this gap for three weeks of recipe-tuning; the audit was triggered by P13's γ verdict. See [`viso_bucket_gap`](../threads/viso_bucket_gap.md).

The clean takeaway for a future agent: **P13 was directionally correct and instrumentation-clean, but the substrate-redesign approach is bounded by the bucket gap and the residual shortcut both. The three structural bugs above mean that the verdict reflects the recipe's true behavior on the second-generation infrastructure — every prior P-series scorecard read pre-fix should be re-checked against `selected_threshold` before any conclusion stands.**
