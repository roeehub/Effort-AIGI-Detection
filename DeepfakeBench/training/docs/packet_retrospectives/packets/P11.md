**Critical artifact**: trainer's `top_n` (AUC-ranked), `ood_composite`, and `value_composite` save policies all converged on **step 1000 only** for every P11 run. Step 1000 is the WORST anchor moment for all 3 SUCCEEDED runs. The trainer's AUC-ranked save policy froze on the worst possible checkpoint. **The post-step-1000 anchor improvements visible in W&B (HEAVY's anchor_composite reached 0.529 at step 6000) were never persisted as checkpoints.** AUC saturated at step 1000, so AUC-ranked saves froze. The yaml had `save_every_steps: 1000` but **that key is not recognized by trainer.py** — silent no-op. Triggers the `periodic_saves` patch (commit `c7dc828`) that becomes the next-packet (P12) load-bearing fix.

## Conclusions drawn in-session

- **The face-size + codec axis intervention works directionally** — HEAVY recipe (`context_variation_scale=0.50`, real_codec_uplift, teams_codec_sim_p=0.65) beat P8A on per-method recall at trainer-side. The 4× FPR cost is structural to the saved checkpoint (step 1000 = WORST anchor moment), not to the recipe — HEAVY's anchor_mean continuously improved through step 6000. **Implication for P12**: extend HEAVY's training schedule to step 8000 with periodic checkpoint saves at 1000-step granularity to capture the recovery. (LOG `:1213-1234`).
- **WEBCAM_HARDEN aug primitive is too aggressive** — NaN at step 5683. The MotionBlur + ImageCompression(18-50) + GaussNoise(20-70 var) chain compounds; needs separate debugging. Don't ship as-is. (LOG `:1169`).
- **HEAVY_DEEPLIVE 6× weight backfires** — deeplive recall regresses; aggressive sampling breaks pos/neg pair structure for contrastive negatives. Stay at 3.0. (LOG `:1170-1171`).
- **The trainer's checkpoint policy is a load-bearing bug.** AUC-ranked saves freeze on the WORST anchor moment (step 1000) when AUC saturates early. The yaml's `save_every_steps: 1000` is silently not consumed (recognized keys: `top_n_top_n_size`, `ood_composite_top_n_size`, `value_composite_top_n_size`). **Action: implement `periodic_saves` block.** (LOG `:1173-1190`).
- **Modern_lockbox_v2 is the deployment-relevant FPR axis.** P8A baseline 4.59% → 0.71% under v2 filter at the same τ; the data-hygiene story is now empirically clean (memory `project_lockbox_fpr_dominated_by_webcam_mode.md`). Day-2 readout uses v2 alongside baseline. (LOG `:1100-1133`).
