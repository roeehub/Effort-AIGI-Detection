# Thread: Promotion contract evolution

## The question

What metric should decide which checkpoint is ready to deploy? Through the relaunch the team moved from a training-side composite (AUC-ish, then `value_composite`) to a dev-calibrated lexicographic τ with a lockbox readout. The question is not "what should the model optimize during training" — it is "what readout actually reflects the deployment risk we care about."

## Initial belief

[WT-C/D/E](../packets/WT_infrastructure.md) (April 17) set up the machinery assuming the fixed-0.5 scorecard and an FPR-minimization wrapper would be sufficient, with WT-D's threshold sweeps and WT-E's eight-suite manifest carrying the decision. Through [RLP1](../packets/RLP1.md) and [RLP2](../packets/RLP2.md) selection was on `best_ood_composite` (AUC-based), treated as adequate for directional decisions.

## What changed our mind

- **2026-04-17 WT-E scaffold (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/WT-E_2026-04-17.md:8-70`)**: the eight-suite manifest landed at `arena/target_domain_suites.teams_promotion_contract_2026-04-17.yaml` (incl. previously-claimed-missing `teams_fake_all_lockbox`); the five-checkpoint shortlist froze at `R12_G_FP32`, `R13_A_STEP15500`, `R13_E_BESTSOFAR`, `R13_FT7_FP32`, `R13_FT9_FP32` (`arena/checkpoint_maps/teams_target_domain.promotion_shortlist_2026-04-17.yaml`). The fixed-`0.5` scorecard wrappers were **demoted to diagnostic-only** (`docs/TRACK_C_PROMOTION_CONTRACT_RUNBOOK_2026-04-17.md:17-31`). WT-E shipped local (`arena/run_teams_promotion_contract.sh`) and Vertex (`arena/launch_teams_promotion_contract.sh`) wrappers, but **deferred emitting `promotion_winner.json` on the shortlist** — the fact that real readouts had not been run was acknowledged at merge time (`WT-E_2026-04-17.md:55-63`).
- **2026-04-17 WT-D minimal-baseline scoping** (`docs/WT_D_MINIMAL_BASELINE_PACKET_2026-04-17.md:60-93`, `docs/relaunch_handoffs/WT-D_2026-04-17.md:42-44`): WT-D explicitly admitted *"this runtime still cannot name a calibrated five-checkpoint promotion winner because the shortlist report artifacts are not locally accessible here."* The WT-D primitives (`tools/teams_video_policy_analysis.py`, `tools/teams_frame_policy_analysis.py`, threshold/abstain/temporal/hysteresis tooling, `tests/test_teams_{video,frame}_policy_analysis.py`) shipped working — but with no actual ranked five-checkpoint readout. The contract-policy bug class that later surfaces (FPR-minimization no-budget driving τ to ~0.995) lives in this layer's wrapper, not in the tools themselves.
- **2026-04-19→04-21 RLP1/RLP2 selected on `best_ood_composite` only — promotion contract not in path.** RLP1 (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET1_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-19.md:241-247`) explicitly *"the comparison reference remains `R12_G_FP32`, but only through the current frozen target-domain evaluation contract. Do not compare tonight's results to stale historical logged numbers. Rescore the incumbent and all packet winners on the same current scorecard lane."* RLP1 and RLP2 then never run the WT-E shortlist scorer — both packets are scored on training-side `best_ood_composite` checkpoints (an AUC-style composite of holdout + OOD AUCs), per [RLP1.md:36-49](../packets/RLP1.md) and [RLP2.md:42-53](../packets/RLP2.md). The April-21 source-of-truth handoff (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_TRAINING_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_2026-04-21.md:97-113`) lists "running the WT-E promotion contract if model promotion is needed" as **optional** parallel work. The structural consequence: through Slice 2 the FPR-minimization no-budget bug (loop `fpr-minimization-no-budget-tau-collapse`) is structurally present in merged code but has no live evidence yet, because RLP1/RLP2 leaders never get τ-calibrated. RLP3's A9 introduction of `value_composite` (Slice 3) is the first time an in-trainer composite tries to track the deployment readout — and is also the first time the deferred contract bites.
- **2026-04-21 prediction-stability becomes a packet-level lever (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET2_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-21.md:30-42, 158-197`).** RLP2 introduces explicit `ood/score_jitter/*` monitoring and a required post-train WT-D frame-policy pass for the top 2-3 RLP2 checkpoints. The decision-system tooling that WT-D shipped without a calibrated readout in Slice 1 is now back in path as a stability surface, not as a promotion winner. The handoff is explicit that *"do not treat a run as a stability win just because one jitter number moves down if the packet collapses or the main balanced metric falls apart"* — i.e., jitter is informational, not a promotion gate. Live RLP2 jitter readings (`RLP1_01` YT 0.04286 / Teams real 0.03946 / Teams fake 0.08373; `RLP1_04` 0.04038 / 0.04862 / 0.08640; `RLP1_05` 0.04113 / 0.05464 / 0.08480) show heavier proper-data hurts Teams-real jitter even when composite is healthier — first numeric evidence the AUC-only metric is missing a deployment-relevant axis.
- **[RLP3](../packets/RLP3.md) A9 introduced `value_composite`** = `0.6 * teams_fakes_tpr + 0.3 * other_fakes_tpr + 0.1 * stability` at the τ meeting `mean_FPR ≤ 0.02 AND max_FPR ≤ 0.04` across 6 real pools. Session `99732ab8`: pulling `best_value_composite` instead of `best_ood_composite` **flipped the packet-3 ranking** and showed all runs already past their peak. Dual-checkpoint A2 was the only reason this was visible.
- **[RLP3.5](../packets/RLP3_5.md) gate-definition shift** (mean_fpr 0.02→0.03, max_fpr 0.04→0.05, jitter max→p95) shrunk the arcface-m=0.15 lead from `+0.11` to `+0.021` — the gate move was doing more mass than the training knob. First clean in-session separation of "metric moved the number" from "training moved the number".
- **[RLP6](../packets/RLP6.md) corrected gate-block model.** `worst_pool_fpr` had been assumed to include fake pools (the basis for dropping `wma_failure_fake`). Reading `trainer/trainer.py:161-216` showed it is **real-pool-only**. The real gate drivers were avspeech (54.2%/45.8% FPR) and VCD — not deployment-distribution pools — force-pushing τ to ~0.995 and crushing fake recall. Dropping them unlocked +0.127 on the composite with no retraining.
- **FPR-minimization policy bug** (memory `project_contract_policy_bug.md`). When the contract runs "minimize FPR with no budget", it drives τ to ~0.995, crushes fake recall, and the scorecard still "passes" because no explicit recall floor is enforced. Always check `selected_threshold` before trusting the scorecard.
- **[RLP6](../packets/RLP6.md) slot-04 shortcut exposure** (see [processing_signature_shortcut](processing_signature_shortcut.md)). A checkpoint with `value_composite=0.9006` — contract-legal — false-flagged real Teams participants on different cameras. Lockbox 90/90 **not threshold-reachable** at RLP6_04. `value_composite` cannot see the camera/ISP shortcut because the real pools used to compute τ do not cover the failure surface.
- **Trainer's `value_composite` is not deployment-grade.** Memory `project_promotion_contract.md` is authoritative: the deployment readout is dev-calibrated lexicographic τ, then lockbox.

### Slice 3 evidence — value_composite introduction (2026-04-29)

This subsection consolidates the Slice-3 evidence for the `value_composite` lifecycle as it landed in [RLP3](../packets/RLP3.md), [RLP3.5](../packets/RLP3_5.md), and [RLP4](../packets/RLP4.md). The bullets above already reference these findings; this section fills in the dated mechanics so the lineage is auditable.

- **2026-04-21 plan: A9 introduces `value_composite` as readout-only** (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-21.md:224-274`). The metric is defined as `0.6 * teams_fakes_tpr + 0.3 * other_fakes_tpr + 0.1 * stability` at the τ meeting `mean_FPR ≤ 0.02 AND max_FPR ≤ 0.04` across 6 real pools. Critical design choice (`:259-264`): selection **stays on `best_ood_composite`** through RLP3 to preserve packet-2 comparability; `value_composite` is logged via dual-checkpoint A2 (one per criterion) so the counterfactual is visible without breaking the AUC-comparable history. The packet-4 switch-criterion is named explicitly: if RLP3 ranking by `value_composite` matches ranking by `final_eval/ood_composite` to within `σ_seed`, AND `final_eval/agree/same_step` is true for most slots, RLP4 adopts `value_composite` as the selection metric.
- **2026-04-22 RLP3 retro-score: `value_composite` flips slot ranking** (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_RETRO_SCORE_RESULTS_2026-04-22.md:77-83`). Under the NEW gate `(0.03, 0.05, p95)`, RLP3 retro-scored slots produced: 02 main `0.7027` / 04 spatial `0.7215` / 05 low_arc+spatial `0.7232`. The legacy gate ranked slot 04 above slot 05 (0.6237 vs 0.6224); the new gate flipped it (0.7232 vs 0.7215). The flip is inside σ_seed noise — both readings agree the spatial-stack and low-ArcFace+spatial-stack tie at the top. Session `99732ab8`: pulling `best_value_composite` instead of `best_ood_composite` flipped the packet's ranking and showed *"all runs are already past their peak"* — dual-checkpoint A2 is what made this readable. Without A2, the team would have selected on AUC and never seen the value-composite peak land at a different step.
- **2026-04-22 RLP3.5 gate-definition shift makes the metric live** (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_5_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-22.md:37-42, 60-67`). Three knobs change globally: `target_mean_fpr 0.02 → 0.03`, `max_pool_fpr 0.04 → 0.05`, `stability_jitter_stat max → p95`. The relaxation is user-approved as deployment-defensible. Effect: RLP3.5 slot 02 (`m=0.15`) clears `+0.0415` over retro-P3_02 under the matched new gate, but only `+0.021` over retro-P3 leader RLP3_05 (`0.7232`). The "~5× smaller deltas" observation ([RLP3_5.md:62](../packets/RLP3_5.md), session `1a4ce3aa`) is the first clean in-session signal that **gate-definition was moving as much mass as training-knob choices** — a load-bearing data point for this thread (separates "metric moved the number" from "training moved the number"). Stability term jumps from `0.0008` (max-aggregated, sanity slot 02) to `0.4475` (p95-aggregated, NEW slot 02) on the same checkpoint — the aggregator swap, not the threshold relaxation, is what unpinned the stability term (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_RETRO_SCORE_RESULTS_2026-04-22.md:87`).
- **2026-04-22 max-FPR gate in production: slot RLP35_03 `m=0.20` blocked** ([RLP3_5.md:52, 73](../packets/RLP3_5.md)). The slot sat at `worst_pool_fpr ≈ 0.055` for 22+ hours and never cleared the (relaxed) 0.05 ceiling — first instance of a contract-side gate refusing to admit a checkpoint whose training signal was otherwise plausible. The pattern ("checkpoint with strong training signal unpromotable purely because a deployment gate declined") is the [gate_alignment_story](gate_alignment_story.md) in miniature; see that thread for the same pattern at scale in [RLP6](../packets/RLP6.md).
- **2026-04-22 retro-runner sanity drift: `−0.0067` is systematic** (`docs/relaunch_handoffs/R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_RETRO_SCORE_RESULTS_2026-04-22.md:30-58`). The handoff spec called for `≤1e-3`; observed is `6.7e-3`. Causes considered: non-determinism in OOD sampler / identity-split / frame order; augmentation RNG for stress OOD lanes; GPU kernel determinism. Since the drift is the same pipeline for all 4 retro runs (sanity + 3 NEW-gate), it is **systematic** — `Δ = P3.5_best − P3_top_retro_new` cancels both sides approximately, but the systematic floor remains a known caveat for any single retro-score number quoted. The user weighed this and approved proceeding with the NEW-gate retro launches. Loop tracked separately in [RLP3.md](../packets/RLP3.md) "Open loops" — the root cause was never fully traced through Slice 3.
- **2026-04-22 `value_composite` config propagation bug: silent legacy-fallback if not re-applied in `train_sweep.py`.** RLP3.5 slots 01/02 launched under image `1.3.192` and the trainer init logs printed legacy `(0.02 / 0.04 / max)` despite the yamls declaring the new block. Root cause: `wandb.init` flattens nested dicts; `train_sweep.py` must explicitly copy the `value_composite` block from `single_cfg` into `config` before launching the trainer. Fix in commit `872502c` (8 lines added to the existing per-block re-apply allowlist; image bumped `1.3.192 → 1.3.193`). Without the fix, every RLP3.5 new-gate run would have silently used legacy gates. See thread [`wandb_yaml_propagation_bugs`](wandb_yaml_propagation_bugs.md) for the full bug-class lifecycle. The retro runner (`retro_score_value_composite.py`) had to be patched separately for the same logical change — same class of bug, distinct code path.
- **2026-04-22 RLP4: contract metric carried forward by procedural failure**. RLP4 was supposed to test the packet-4 switch-criterion (does selection on `value_composite` produce different leaders than `best_ood_composite` on a controlled m=0.125/0.15/0.175 × control/spatial scan?). All 8 RLP4 jobs failed at startup with `FileNotFoundError` (yamls not in image; see thread [`image_rebuild_discipline`](image_rebuild_discipline.md)). The contract-side switch question was not answered in this packet; RLP5 carried `m=0.15 + spatial` forward on RLP3.5 evidence alone and did not re-test the selection-metric switch. Through Slice 3, the dual-checkpoint A2 logging continues, but no packet has formally re-evaluated the switch criterion stated in `R13_RELAUNCH_PACKET3_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-04-21.md:259-264`.

The Slice-3 close on `value_composite`: it survived as a directional in-trainer metric, the gate-definition shift exposed how much mass the gate moves vs the training knob, the policy-bug class (silent legacy-fallback) was caught and fixed once, and the procedural failure of RLP4 deferred the formal selection-metric switch. The promotion-contract structural bug `fpr-minimization-no-budget-tau-collapse` did **not** first surface in Slice 3 — RLP3 retro-scored τ values are in the 0.96-0.97 range, not the 0.995 collapse range. The first live evidence of τ-tail collapse belongs to a later slice.

### Slice 4 evidence — the τ-tail-collapse goes live (2026-04-29)

This subsection consolidates the Slice-4 evidence: it is the first slice where the `fpr-minimization-no-budget-tau-collapse` policy bug produces a numeric, in-the-wild scorecard, and where `value_composite` definitively loses its claim to deployment-grade status.

The slot-07 sanity readout is the first time both halves of that disagreement are visible in the same artifact: trainer-side `value_composite=0.7736` (later interpreted as gate-bound by RLP6) vs contract-side macro recall `9.7%`. The Slice-4 close on `value_composite` and the contract: (1) the policy bug `fpr-minimization-no-budget-tau-collapse` has its first live evidence (slot-07 sanity 2026-04-23), so the loop moves from `open` to `in-progress` with `last_verified=2026-04-29`; (2) `value_composite` is now operationally not-deployment-grade with RLP6_04 as the load-bearing concrete proof; (3) the deployment readout converges to lockbox + per-identity reducer, but a contract-yaml level fix that prevents the τ-tail-collapse remains absent.
