## 1. Method

1. Load `unified_frame_matrix.csv`. Filter `label==0` and drop rows with any null in {`min_dim`, `lap_var`, `luma_mean`, `saturation_mean`, `color_a_dev`, `color_b_dev`} → n=3,884 frames.
2. Define `identity := basename(frame_path).split('__')[0]` (65 unique identities in the filtered real subset).
3. **B1**: group by identity, compute `n_frames`, `is_chronic_6` (max over frames), mean and p90 of {P8A, T5C_step3500, T3_S1_step1500}, mean of each of the 6 IQ axes. Write `outputs/per_identity_aggregates.csv` for all 65 identities.
4. Filter to identities with `n_frames ≥ 30` → 11 identities (5 chronic_6, 6 non-chronic). All downstream regressions use this filtered set.
5. **B2**: per ckpt, transform identity mean(score) → mean-logit via `logit(m) = log(clip(m,eps,1-eps)/(1-clip(m,eps,1-eps)))` with `eps = 1e-3`. Z-score the 6 identity-mean IQ axes (population SD, ddof=0). Fit closed-form OLS `logit ~ z(IQ_axes) + intercept`. Report `R²`, per-axis standardized β with 95% CI from `t.ppf(0.975, dof)` where `dof = n - (k+1) = 11 - 7 = 4`. (Note on tooling: local `statsmodels` import is broken due to a scipy compat issue; OLS is computed in closed form using `numpy.linalg.pinv` plus `scipy.stats.t` for the t-critical value.)
6. **B3**: per ckpt, rank identities by `|observed_logit - predicted_logit|` desc; emit top 10 with `{identity, n_frames, is_chronic_6, observed_mean_score, predicted_mean_score, observed_logit, predicted_logit, residual_logit}`.

## 6. Caveats

1. **n=11 identities, dof=4**. With 6 predictors plus intercept, every per-axis 95% CI in §3.1 straddles zero. The joint R² values are reported as-is; the per-axis β decomposition is unstable to collinearity at this sample size and should not be read as identifying a unique "responsible" axis for any ckpt.
2. **IQ-atlas join coverage is ~50.6%** (inherited from Stage A). 3,884 of 9,077 real frames have non-null IQ. The 6,731 dropped real rows are not random — they are biased by whichever bucket the IQ extractor did not cover (Stage A docs).
3. **Identity extraction**: `basename(path).split('__')[0]` produces some single-frame identities for `dor_shkedi_real_frame_*` paths (54 of 65 unique identities have n_frames=1). The `n_frames ≥ 30` filter excludes all of these. Inside the 11-identity regression set, no per-frame mis-split occurs.
4. **Mean-logit transform** uses `eps=1e-3`. For the 11 retained identities the smallest observed mean is `Cam_Test` P8A = 0.0061 (logit −5.10); the largest is Roy_D T5C = 0.9004 (logit +2.20). Clipping does not bind on any retained identity.
5. **Identity ≠ frame-level conditioning**. R² values here speak to between-identity variance only. A high (or low) per-identity R² does not pin down whether within-identity per-frame score variance is IQ-driven.
6. **`is_chronic_6` and identity are nearly co-extensive in this 11-id set** (5/11 are chronic_6, and the chronic_6 flag is a property of identity in the source data). The regression does not include `is_chronic_6` as a covariate; its effect is absorbed into the per-identity mean.

## 7. Direct observations

1. After the `n_frames ≥ 30` filter, the regression set has **11 identities** (5 chronic_6, 6 non-chronic), derived from **3,884 real frames** with all 6 IQ axes non-null out of **9,077 real frames** in the Stage A unified matrix.
2. P8A joint R² across identities is **0.2223**; top-|β| axis is **color_a_dev** at β_std = **+1.393** (95% CI [−12.18, +14.96]).
3. T5C_step3500 joint R² across identities is **0.5978**; top-|β| axis is **saturation_mean** at β_std = **+3.511** (95% CI [−10.44, +17.47]).
4. T3_S1_step1500 joint R² across identities is **0.4902**; top-|β| axis is **saturation_mean** at β_std = **+4.419** (95% CI [−19.11, +27.95]).
5. The top-residual identity for P8A is **real_dor** (residual_logit = **−2.325**; observed_mean 0.0084, predicted_mean 0.0793; n=43; chronic_6=0).
6. The top-residual identity for T5C_step3500 is **Cam_Test** (residual_logit = **−1.330**; observed_mean 0.0831, predicted_mean 0.2553; n=34; chronic_6=0).
7. The top-residual identity for T3_S1_step1500 is **real_dor** (residual_logit = **−2.256**; observed_mean 0.0165, predicted_mean 0.1379; n=43; chronic_6=0).
8. On all 3 ckpts, the sign of the residual partitions the 11 identities along the chronic_6 flag in identical proportions: **5 of 5 chronic_6 identities have positive residual** (observed > IQ-predicted) and **5 of 6 non-chronic identities have negative residual** (observed < IQ-predicted) (§4.4). The single non-chronic identity with positive residual is `xiang` on every ckpt.
9. Three identities recur in the top-5 |residual| of all 3 ckpts: **real_dor**, **Cam_Test**, **PC_Generator**. Two additional identities recur in the top-5 of 2/3 ckpts: **Roy_D** and **bla_bla_chow**.
10. The signs of these recurring residuals are stable across ckpts: real_dor and Cam_Test are observed-below-predicted on all 3 ckpts; PC_Generator, Roy_D, and bla_bla_chow are observed-above-predicted on all 3 ckpts.
11. Per-axis 95% CIs for every (ckpt × axis) cell in §3.1 straddle zero. At n=11 identities and 6 predictors, the per-axis β decomposition is not individually distinguishable from zero at α=0.05.
