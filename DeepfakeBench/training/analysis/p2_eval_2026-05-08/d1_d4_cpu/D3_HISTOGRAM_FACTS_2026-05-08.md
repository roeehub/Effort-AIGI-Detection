## Question

What do the per-frame `prob_fake` distributions look like across the 5 selected P2
ckpts (A/top_n_step500, B/top_n_step500, C/top_n_step7000, D/top_n_step6000,
D/top_n_step19000) and P8A reference, broken down by cohort?

## Method

For each cohort × ckpt combination, compute count + summary statistics (mean, std,
p05, p25, p50, p75, p95, min, max). Render overlaid step-histograms (40 bins on [0,1])
per cohort and per global label. PNG output at `figs/d3_hist_<cohort>.png` and
`figs/d3_hist_label_{0,1}.png`.

## Direct observations (numeric only)

1. Slot A and Slot B `top_n_step500` produce narrow score distributions (per-cohort
   spread within their own ckpt is 0.20-0.27); means cluster in [0.32, 0.63].
2. Slot C, D-step6000, D-step19000 produce wide bimodal distributions on every cohort
   (per-cohort min-max spread > 0.40 within a single ckpt for most cohorts).
3. P8A reference is bimodal too; P8A's per-cohort means span [0.007, 0.928].
4. PC_Generator__s22: P2 ckpts vary 0.078 (C step7000) to 0.785 (D step19000) —
   spread 0.71. P8A scores it 0.919.
5. PC_Generator__s45: ckpt spread 0.494-0.768 across all 5 P2 ckpts; P8A 0.675 (within range).
6. Q__s6: P2 ckpts 0.222-0.555; P8A 0.928 (3-4× higher than every P2 ckpt).
7. Roy_D: C/D ckpts all > 0.93; A/B ckpts 0.53/0.60; P8A 0.606.
8. bla_bla_chow: every P2 ckpt > 0.52 (most > 0.7); P8A 0.102.
9. healthy_dor, healthy_test_cam, healthy_md_noyn_sharker: C/D-step6k/D-step19k all
   under 0.07; A/B in 0.32-0.58 range; P8A under 0.11.
10. deeplive_fake: C/D ckpts all >0.95; A/B in 0.50-0.56 range; P8A 0.461.
11. lockbox_fake: C 0.703, D-step6k 0.688, D-step19k 0.774; A/B 0.510/0.583; P8A 0.629.
12. viso_fake: D-step19k 0.690 highest, D-step6k 0.484 lowest among C/D ckpts; P8A 0.373.
